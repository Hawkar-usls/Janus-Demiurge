JANUS_ANCHOR_EVAL_VERSION=1
PURPOSE=FROZEN_COMPARABILITY_BENCHMARK_NOT_TRAINING_SOURCE
AUTHORITY=EVALUATION_ONLY

<JANUS_ANCHOR_RECORD id="A01">
OBSERVE -> FREEZE -> REFERENCE -> BRIDGE -> RECONSTRUCT -> VERIFY -> PRESERVE.
Evidence is preserved before interpretation. A claim does not gain authority from repetition.
</JANUS_ANCHOR_RECORD>

<JANUS_ANCHOR_RECORD id="A02">
SILENCE != NEGATIVE_EVIDENCE. NO_RECEIPT != VERIFY_FAIL. STALE_EVIDENCE != VERIFY_FAIL.
Unknown states remain unknown until a qualifying witness resolves them.
</JANUS_ANCHOR_RECORD>

<JANUS_ANCHOR_RECORD id="A03">
VERIFY_PASS != GLOBAL_TRUTH. A verifier establishes only the bounded contract it actually tested.
Target-local verification does not grant unrelated repository authority.
</JANUS_ANCHOR_RECORD>

<JANUS_ANCHOR_RECORD id="A04">
CODE_PRESENCE != RUNTIME_EXECUTION. WORKFLOW_SUCCESS != MODEL_CORRECTNESS.
Runtime claims require runtime receipts; source inspection remains source inspection.
</JANUS_ANCHOR_RECORD>

<JANUS_ANCHOR_RECORD id="A05">
NEXUS_READ_INSPECT != WRITE_AUTHORITY. Evidence edges are not authority edges.
Read-only organization knowledge may inform a proposal but may not mutate its target.
</JANUS_ANCHOR_RECORD>

<JANUS_ANCHOR_RECORD id="A06">
PROPOSAL -> TARGET_LOCAL_VERIFIER -> SEALED_RECEIPT. A proposal is not an execution grant.
Mutation remains local, explicit, bounded, reversible, and attributable.
</JANUS_ANCHOR_RECORD>

<JANUS_ANCHOR_RECORD id="A07">
REJECTED_PROVENANCE != NEGATIVE_TRAINING_SIGNAL. TAMPERED evidence is quarantined, not learned as failure.
Training eligibility requires intact lineage and explicit verifier semantics.
</JANUS_ANCHOR_RECORD>

<JANUS_ANCHOR_RECORD id="A08">
A durable decision records checkpoint identity, evidence identity, source run, and predecessor identity.
A stale writer must never silently replace a newer durable decision.
</JANUS_ANCHOR_RECORD>

<JANUS_ANCHOR_RECORD id="A09">
SCOUT observes a target HEAD. If the target HEAD changes, refresh evidence and recompute the proposal.
Never weaken exact-base and never automatically rebase an old proposal onto a new target.
</JANUS_ANCHOR_RECORD>

<JANUS_ANCHOR_RECORD id="A10">
Scheduled wake, push, workflow_run, and manual dispatch are distinct event classes.
Observability activity and model activity are not natural Git-Life schedule witnesses.
</JANUS_ANCHOR_RECORD>

<JANUS_ANCHOR_RECORD id="A11">
Research has layers: DISCOVERY, CORROBORATED, TRAINING_ELIGIBLE.
Retrieval can inform context; only an explicit frozen gate may authorize a training signal.
</JANUS_ANCHOR_RECORD>

<JANUS_ANCHOR_RECORD id="A12">
The incumbent and challenger see the same frozen evidence snapshot during comparison.
A shadow challenger has no actuation authority before promotion gates are satisfied.
</JANUS_ANCHOR_RECORD>

<JANUS_ANCHOR_RECORD id="A13">
A changing adaptive corpus measures current adaptation. A frozen anchor measures comparability over time.
Neither metric alone is sufficient for safe autonomous promotion.
</JANUS_ANCHOR_RECORD>

<JANUS_ANCHOR_RECORD id="A14">
Candidate improvement on the current holdout cannot hide material regression on the frozen anchor.
Promotion is conjunctive: ADAPTIVE_GATE AND ANCHOR_GATE.
</JANUS_ANCHOR_RECORD>

<JANUS_ANCHOR_RECORD id="A15">
Every evaluation record binds corpus digest, anchor digest, model config, seed, and checkpoint lineage.
Two numbers without their evaluation identity are not a valid historical comparison.
</JANUS_ANCHOR_RECORD>

<JANUS_ANCHOR_RECORD id="A16">
A replay memory accepts explicit verified outcomes and deduplicates receipt identity.
Repeated ingestion of one receipt must not increase its learning weight twice.
</JANUS_ANCHOR_RECORD>

<JANUS_ANCHOR_RECORD id="A17">
Replay is stratified by target organ and verifier profile. Influence remains bounded.
A learned prior cannot override a hard verifier rejection or remove the option to abstain.
</JANUS_ANCHOR_RECORD>

<JANUS_ANCHOR_RECORD id="A18">
The model may propose. VERIFY decides whether the bounded proposal passed its local contract.
The model's own reflection is not self-validating evidence.
</JANUS_ANCHOR_RECORD>

<JANUS_ANCHOR_RECORD id="A19">
Memory is append-evidenced and latest-state updates are monotonic.
Conflicting writers preserve both candidates and expose the conflict instead of erasing history.
</JANUS_ANCHOR_RECORD>

<JANUS_ANCHOR_RECORD id="A20">
The authority ceiling is explicit: no secrets, no admin, no permission elevation, no autonomous merge.
Learning does not imply authority expansion.
</JANUS_ANCHOR_RECORD>

<JANUS_ANCHOR_RECORD id="A21">
UNKNOWN_RESOURCE remains an unknown resource. Missing access is not evidence that the resource is empty or false.
A failed read cannot be converted into semantic negative evidence.
</JANUS_ANCHOR_RECORD>

<JANUS_ANCHOR_RECORD id="A22">
A source record carries path, content hash, byte length, source commit, and provenance class.
Derived interpretation remains distinguishable from source-bearing evidence.
</JANUS_ANCHOR_RECORD>

<JANUS_ANCHOR_RECORD id="A23">
The current model checkpoint is content-addressed. Its parent checkpoint is content-addressed.
Lineage is reproducible when the same evaluation contracts and evidence snapshots are available.
</JANUS_ANCHOR_RECORD>

<JANUS_ANCHOR_RECORD id="A24">
Fail closed on malformed schemas, missing mandatory return fields, hash mismatch, and authority leakage.
Do not manufacture a PASS from a partially observed execution.
</JANUS_ANCHOR_RECORD>

<JANUS_ANCHOR_RECORD id="A25">
A verified local success is useful experience, not proof of universal correctness.
Generalization is tested by independent future evidence, not declared by the learner.
</JANUS_ANCHOR_RECORD>

<JANUS_ANCHOR_RECORD id="A26">
Persistent memory survives a workflow run. Ephemeral artifacts may carry evidence into a durable writer.
A run that thinks but does not retrain may still persist its bounded decision receipt.
</JANUS_ANCHOR_RECORD>

<JANUS_ANCHOR_RECORD id="A27">
Training can be skipped when the source digest is unchanged. Decision and observability may still run.
Skipping unnecessary gradient updates is not equivalent to skipping cognition or persistence.
</JANUS_ANCHOR_RECORD>

<JANUS_ANCHOR_RECORD id="A28">
A natural schedule witness must identify event_name, workflow_ref, run_id, run_attempt, head_sha, and time.
Manual rerun identity must not be counted as a new natural wake.
</JANUS_ANCHOR_RECORD>

<JANUS_ANCHOR_RECORD id="A29">
Fresh evidence is preferred to weakening safety constraints. Refresh Scout before recomputing action.
Exact-base remains a safety invariant even when it causes abstention.
</JANUS_ANCHOR_RECORD>

<JANUS_ANCHOR_RECORD id="A30">
LEARN FROM VERIFIED OUTCOMES, NOT FROM SELF-BELIEF.
KEEP EVIDENCE FRESH, EVALUATION COMPARABLE, MEMORY MONOTONIC, AND AUTHORITY LOCAL.
</JANUS_ANCHOR_RECORD>
