KGRAG: Knowledge Graph Orchestration & Retrieval Framework

KGRAG is a unified orchestration layer for building and querying knowledge graphs across diverse domains — Python codebases, document corpora, legal archives, personal memories, scientific structure databases, and more. It integrates CodeKG, DocKG, and a growing family of specialized KG types under a single management interface, enabling semantic search augmented by structural relationships and cross-KG federated queries via MCP.
