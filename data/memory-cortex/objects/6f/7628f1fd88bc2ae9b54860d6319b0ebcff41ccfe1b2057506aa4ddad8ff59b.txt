Run-to-failure simulations of 480 geared turbofan engines under recorded flight conditions

Run-to-failure simulations of 480 engines of NASA's AGTF30 geared turbofan model (30,000 lbf class). Each engine flies, second by second from new to end of life, recorded flights taken from the N-CMAPSS dataset. Engines degrade through build wear, background wear and an exponentially growing fault. Eight subsets of 60 engines cover the faulty-component combinations of the seven N-CMAPSS failure modes and one further combination. End of life is the first flight after which the temperature margin at a fixed take-off check is used up. The 37,270 flights hold operating conditions, measured signals with and without noise, virtual and controller signals, and per-flight health ground truth, in the N-CMAPSS file layout. A fixed development/test split (336/144 engines) and nested training subsets are included. See README.md and the data card in the index zip files.
