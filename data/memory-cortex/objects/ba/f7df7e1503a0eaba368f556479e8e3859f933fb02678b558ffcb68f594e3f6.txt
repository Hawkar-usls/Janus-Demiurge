SAT Solving for Power Peak Minimization in the Parallel--Serial--with--Crossover Assembly Line Balancing Problem

The Parallel--Serial-with-Crossover Assembly Line Balancing Problem with Power Peak Minimization (PSCALB3PM) was introduced by Delorme and Gianessi (2022), who proposed an integer linear programming (ILP) model, and further developed in 2024 with a multi-start local-search matheuristic. This paper presents an exact approach based on Boolean satisfiability (SAT) that combines a compact encoding with incremental SAT solving. Specifically, it develops a compact encoding of task-to-workstation precedence constraints and an incremental SAT-based optimization procedure for power peak minimization. We evaluate the proposed method on 375 benchmark instances and compare it with both reference methods. SAT finds feasible solutions for all 375 instances and certifies optimality for 159, compared with 346 feasible solutions and 126 optimality certificates obtained by the ILP method. Relative to the reference matheuristic, SAT achieves lower power peaks on 130 instances, whereas the matheuristic obtains lower values on only four. These findings establish SAT as a competitive exact method for PSCALB3PM, achieving higher feasible-solution coverage and more optimality proofs than the reference ILP model, while generally outperforming the existing matheuristic in terms of solution quality.
