High-Dimensional Order Structure and Approximation Theory of Power-Affine Compositions

[Objective] We develop a high-dimensional counterpart of one-dimensional power-affine composition under the coordinatewise product order, and study its approximation and algebraic structure. [Methods] We use diagonal dilation groups and multivariate Mellin analysis, Lie brackets of coordinate power fields and cross-coordinate power-affine (HPA) shears, finite-grid scanning, mixed logarithmic derivatives, Hessian ranks, and endpoint valuations. [Results] We identify the characters of diagonal dilations and the Lie brackets of cross-coordinate HPA shears. Positive monotone factors preserve the product order, and finite-grid constructions yield uniform density on compact boxes for continuous nonnegative coordinatewise nondecreasing targets, with explicit resource bounds. We establish continuous separability criteria, common-power uniqueness for one hidden coordinate, cancellation classifications for shared-affine-base direction-matched multipower layers, and identification of non-affine ridge directions. A mixed-Hessian rank invariant gives first-hidden-width lower bounds at arbitrary finite depth. Further results include a multidimensional pre-Schwarzian cocycle, global injectivity criteria from Jacobian conditions, rigidity of order-preserving inverses, two-sided endpoint valuations, and whole-orthant approximation for finite products of bounded nonnegative nondecreasing channel responses. [Limitations] Cancellation classification beyond the shared-affine-base direction-matched multipower subclass, general multilayer parameter identification and inverse structure, density for general targets with cross-coordinate interactions in logarithmic C^r topology for r ≥ 1, and optimal complexity remain open. [Conclusions] Power-affine compositions under the coordinatewise product order form a high-dimensional nonlinear approximation system with order preservation, multiplicative interactions, and a structured composition algebra.
