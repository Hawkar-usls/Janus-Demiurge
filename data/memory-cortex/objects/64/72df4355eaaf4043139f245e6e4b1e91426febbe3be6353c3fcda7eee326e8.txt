Zenodo research description This tenth Classic 777 family investigates the temporal evolution of post-ischemic niches, peri-infarct neurovascular overlap, historical connection identity, inflammation and matrix association, counterfactual attribution, traumatic interface mechanics, axonal continuity, hemorrhage evidence, glial state and network prediction, and independent structural, metabolic and electrophysiological validation. The ten books develop 100 analytical subtopics across 300 candidate equation entries, 82 chapters and 200 Research Gates per book. The working edition is prospective rather than experimental. Neither an anatomical descriptor nor a metabolic signal nor a favorable network predictor establishes repair. Independent source histories and functional witnesses remain indispensable. The five-stage inquiry proceeds from physical origin to mathematical evolution, rival counterfactuals, extreme offline extrapolation, and evidence convergence; a sixth page in each chapter preserves independent research inheritance. Longitudinal state descriptors, lesion geometry, mechanical ratios, and independent modality statistics are kept separate from functional reconnection. The candidate relations are standard or conditional analytical tools, not new validated laws. Historical attribution requires baseline identity; hemorrhage and waveform comparisons require independent adjudication and artifact references. No human intervention, animal intervention, or live biological manipulation is proposed in this working edition.
