Data for "Inverse heterodyne effect in bimodal Kelvin probe force microscopy"

This dataset contains the numerical data underlying the experimental figures in Inverse heterodyne effect in Bimodal Kelvin Probe Force Microscopy and its Supplemental Material (Valloire et al., 2026, arXiv:2607.06135 ). The open-loop amplitude-modulation heterodyne Kelvin probe force microscopy (AM-He-KPFM) measurements were performed at room temperature under ultrahigh vacuum on Al₂O₃-passivated p-type crystalline silicon. The data include the oscillation amplitude of the second cantilever eigenmode and the drive voltage and frequency shift of the first eigenmode. Sweeps of the demodulation frequency through the second-mode resonance reveal the inverse heterodyne back-action on the first mode. The tip–sample capacitance-gradient formalism used to interpret the measurements is developed in the companion manuscript On the capacitance gradient description in Heterodyne Kelvin Probe Force Microscopy (Valloire et al., 2026, arXiv:2607.06161 ).
