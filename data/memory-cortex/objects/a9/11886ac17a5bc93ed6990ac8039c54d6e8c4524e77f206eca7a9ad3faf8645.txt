Corpus, protocols and analysis code for: Journal and venue-ranking restrictions remove unevenly from a research corpus: A meta-research study of the second-hand fashion literature

Every literature review begins by constructing the corpus it will then read. This record holds the data and code behind "Journal and venue-ranking restrictions remove unevenly from a research corpus: A meta-research study of the second-hand fashion literature" by Tengfei Shao and Masayuki Goto, submitted to PLOS ONE, which measures what ordinary review restrictions remove. The deposit is one zip archive, deposit_jtaer_metaresearch_R1.zip, with 430 files. It holds the corpus snapshots at each named population (S0, S1 and S2), the two frozen Boolean queries, the retrieval union of 13,504 records with the disposition of each record, the codebook and annotation protocol, the human-coding protocol, instruments, scorers and sampling frames, the coded columns of the returned workbooks as CSV files, the full-text retrieval records, and every analysis script, including the 9,999-draw ablation null. The entry script DEPOSIT_REPRODUCE.py recomputes the main results, the human-coding scores and the bias analysis with python DEPOSIT_REPRODUCE.py --run. The retrieved full texts stay with their publishers, who hold the copyright. The free-text notes columns of the returned coding workbooks, which no reported value uses, are held outside the deposit. The 2025 SCImago Journal Rank and 2025 ABDC releases are third-party lists held by their providers; the deposit carries the scripts that read them and the derived per-work mapping of 623 rows. The deposit licence (CC BY 4.0) covers the material the authors generated. Abstract text remains the copyright of its publishers or authors and is included for checking the retrieval and screening.
