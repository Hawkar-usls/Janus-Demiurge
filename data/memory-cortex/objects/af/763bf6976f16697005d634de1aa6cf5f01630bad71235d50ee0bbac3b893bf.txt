math-conjecture-blitz: proofs and counterexamples for open conjectures

Proofs and counterexamples for open conjectures from recent mathematics papers, one folder per conjecture. Each result comes with a paper written for mathematicians and with checks that run on an ordinary computer: a Lean 4 formalization where there is one, and independent Python scripts otherwise. Zhan–Bie Conjecture 1 (Integers 26 (2026) A15): proved. The permutations sorted by a cyclic-pattern-avoiding stack followed by West's stack are counted by the large Schröder numbers. Lean 4 proof of the count. Zhan–Bie Conjecture 2: proved. The output of the consecutive cyclic stack closest to the identity is unique. Hill–Luo–Trinh–Vindas-Meléndez Conjecture 8.1 (arXiv:2607.15503): Chan disproved it first and Schubr proved the dimensions 3 to 13. This repository gives an independent computation of the second magic coefficient and further counterexamples. Alspach's conjecture on strongly sequenceable abelian groups: partial. Every abelian group of order at most 32 is strongly sequenceable. Lean 4 proofs of a coset family and a deletion lemma. AI use: the language models GPT-6.2 Astra (OpenAI) and Claude Opus 5.5 (Anthropic) did the mathematical work, with GPT-6.2 Sol (OpenAI) sub-agents. Each paper ends with a paragraph that says which model did what. Zexin Lin set the tasks, coordinated the models and checked the results.
