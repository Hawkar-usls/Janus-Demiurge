Knight-Initiative-for-Brain-Resilience/VasQ-assistant: v1.0.0_VasQ

VasQ v1.0.0 is the software release of VasQ, accompanying the manuscript Atlas of regional molecular specialization in the human brain vasculature. VasQ is an AI chat system for exploring a single-nucleus RNA-seq atlas of the human brain vasculature (41 brain regions, about 443,000 nuclei from four donors). It helps users generate hypotheses about blood–brain barrier biology, regional vascular specialization and drug delivery. This release contains: Web application (Django): classifies each question and routes it to the right workflow. Expression statistics are calculated directly from the log-normalized atlas matrix: mean expression, percentage of expressing cells and cell counts. Groups with fewer than 10 cells are excluded. Results come with interactive plots, tables and CSV downloads. A batch mode takes up to 20 questions and saves a PDF report for each. Only aggregate results are passed to the language model, never the matrix or individual-cell records. Evidence retrieval: gene, disease, pathway and drug evidence with source links, including a knowledge-graph retrieval module built on the SPOKE knowledge graph. Evaluation scripts: for query-intent classification and entity extraction, as reported in the manuscript. Docker configurations: for local use and for deployment on Railway. The hosted version is free to use with no login: https://vasq-production-65c6.up.railway.app/vasq/. To run VasQ locally you need Docker, your own OpenAI API key and three expression data files from Figshare (https://figshare.com/projects/Atlas_of_Regional_Molecular_Specialization_in_the_Human_Brain_Vasculature/273930). Setup instructions and example queries are in the README. VasQ is intended for research exploration and hypothesis generation, not clinical decision-making. The software is released under the Apache License 2.0. Its design was informed by scChat and KG-RAG.
