Finance Corpus Bench

FinanceCorpusBench v0.2 is an open golden-evaluation benchmark for financial-domain LLM corpus governance, archived for citation with a persistent DOI. It contains 120 golden samples in four JSONL subsets covering financial QA, Text-to-SQL, compliance judgment, and evaluator conformance testing. Metric naming aligns with YD/T 6486-2025; compliance design cross-references GB/T 45654-2025 and the JR/T financial standards. The deposit includes the dataset card, metric mapping, annotation guidelines, and a clause-level compliance disclosure. All samples are simulated; no real personal data is included. CC BY 4.0.
