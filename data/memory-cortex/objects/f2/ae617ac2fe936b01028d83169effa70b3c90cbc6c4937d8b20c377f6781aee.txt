Q-FORCE: Reproducibility code and evidence for attribution-aware benchmarking of quantum time-series forecasting

Q-FORCE reproducibility archive for an exploratory benchmark of fixed four-qubit feature maps across eight observational time series and three direct forecasting horizons. Includes boundary-purged CPU benchmarking code, classical and quantum controls, archived model scores and predictions, statistical comparisons, IBM hardware result files, shot-noise analysis, figures, and execution instructions. Saved-evidence analyses were rerun successfully; full model retraining and new hardware execution were not performed during archive preparation. Dataset access links and acquisition instructions are included. The CPU and hardware encodings differ, and raw hardware bitstring histograms and contemporaneous calibration snapshots are unavailable.
