Levers and failure modes of the human right to water in water-intensive industries: data and code

Data and code supporting the article "Levers and failure modes of the human right to water in water-intensive industries" (Faure, Desierto, Adams and Müller). The record contains (1) the mapping of 22 human-rights outcomes onto water-security conditions, governance failures and accountability mechanisms; (2) the 14 industrial cases with their context, location and source counts; (3) the 77 process–stakeholder observations with their documented water-security and governance-failure conditions and the keys of the sources behind each condition; (4) the recurrent configurations of each observation; and (5) every table written by the analysis pipeline. The code archive reproduces every figure and number of the article and its Supplementary Information. The coded-segment export of the case studies, which holds the case narratives written by the research team with quotations from copyrighted sources, is not included and is available from the corresponding author under a data-access agreement. See README.md for the contents and a data dictionary.
