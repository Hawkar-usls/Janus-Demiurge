Zenodo research description This fourth Classic 777 family investigates metabolic repair and functional reconnection as two distinct evidence layers. F031–F035 consider oxidant depletion, respiration, lipid oxidation, pH buffering and ATP stock accounting. F036–F040 address conduction latency, evoked event association, directional alignment, excess coincidence and structure-function discordance. A shared evidence architecture transfers only independently qualified quantities with explicit units, references, uncertainty and vetoes. Ten books explore 100 subtopics through 300 candidate equation entries, 82 chapters per book and 200 Research Gates per book. These are prospective expansions of the master blueprint rather than reports of completed neural regeneration experiments. The five-stage inquiry proceeds from physical origin to mathematical evolution, rival counterfactuals, extreme offline extrapolation, and evidence convergence; a sixth page in each chapter preserves independent research inheritance. Structural measurements, metabolic descriptors, and electrophysiological associations are kept separate from functional reconnection. The candidate relations are standard or conditional analytical tools and are not new validated laws. No human intervention, animal intervention, or live biological manipulation is proposed in this working edition.
