ScienceClaw Hackathon 2026: multiscale collagen network, crosslink and organoid simulations: code, data and run logs

Code, data, run logs and write-ups from the MIT ScienceClaw hackathon (30 October – 1 November 2026). The work asks how collagen fibre networks, crosslink chemistry (LOX-derived, glycation/AGEs, dityrosine, transglutaminase, disulfide) and ageing change tissue mechanics, from bonds and fibrils to organoids. It contains: A multiscale model linking fibre networks to tensegrity cells, YAP mechanosignalling and organ read-outs. Network atlases and controlled-topology sweeps. Calibration and held-out tests against measured collagen rheology and 3D traction-force data. Atomistic and coarse-grained molecular-dynamics studies of crosslinked collagen. Quantum-chemistry checks of carbonyl adducts. A simulated map of how collagen gels shape organoid gene expression. Records of the AI reviewer swarms used to critique the work. The project was run by one researcher directing several AI agents (Claude Code, OpenAI Codex, Gemini reviewers) on local and cloud hardware (NVIDIA Brev, Nebius, Google Cloud). Most results are model outputs with stated assumptions, and negative results are kept. Local paths and account identifiers were removed. Files larger than 200 MB and third-party measured data are listed with checksums instead of included. Start with README.md and CONTINUING.md in the archive. The record holds six zip bundles that share one archive root ( scienceclaw-collagen-2026/ ): 01 collagen models and atlases; 02 calibration and validation; 03 ColBuilder molecular study; 04 molecular dynamics and quantum chemistry; 05 network mechanics and materials; 06 reviews, publication and presentation. Unzip them all into one folder to restore the full tree. Blog write-ups: Human–Avian–AI Symbiosis, ScienceClaw Hackathon category .
