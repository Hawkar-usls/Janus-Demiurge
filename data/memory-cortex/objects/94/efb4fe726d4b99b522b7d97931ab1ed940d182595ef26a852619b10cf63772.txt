Opération "Courroie", Algeria, 1959: Revised Sector-Level Dataset and Reproducibility Package

This dataset accompanies the revised article “Counting Disruption: Military Reporting and the Measurement of Weakening in Opération ‘Courroie’, Algeria, 1959,” submitted to War in History . The package contains the complete eleven-unit sector-level analytical dataset used in the revised study, together with the coding rules, reproducibility files, analytical workbook, scripts, and figure-generation materials. The primary attrition measure is the recorded neutralisation total for each French reporting sector. Subsequent Activity (SA) is a composite visibility measure based on engagements, mine incidents, sabotage acts, and documented insurgent presence during the ninety-day post-operational period. The eleven reporting units constitute the complete Courroie sectoral reporting grid used in the analysis. The dataset is designed to reproduce the descriptive comparison reported in the article, including Pearson and Spearman correlations and the sensitivity check excluding K543. It does not constitute a complete person-level transcription of all neutralisation records or a complete event-level census of all post-operational incidents. The revised analytical package does not use a Weighted Neutralisation Index (WNI) as an analytical measure. Rank remains relevant only as a historical feature of French reporting practices. The package is intended for transparent replication of the published sector-level analysis and for methodological scrutiny of how French military reporting converted recorded losses into claims about organisational weakening.
