Scholarly Prompt Lab: The Prompt as Method

The prompt as method. Design, document and examine how scholarly knowledge develops through human–AI interaction. Free and open-source research software for the humanities and social sciences. Scholarly Prompt Lab turns a research question into a structured scholarly prompt using the CONTEXT framework (Conceptual grounding, Operationalisation, Situated knowledge, Traceability, Evaluation, Cross-model examination, Transparency), with analytical procedures drawn from the researcher's discipline and chosen theoretical frameworks. It supports three research modes: single-prompt analysis; four-condition prompt-sensitivity studies; and documented multi-turn research conversations, in which every researcher prompt is preserved verbatim, linked to its response and evaluated. Conversations can be documented with the SPL Research Session Protocol (version 0.1, a draft under study) and stored as Research Session Records with field-level provenance; a record written by the AI system is checked against the original conversation, and every difference is kept. The Lab checks every quotation in a model's output against the researcher's own sources, suggests evaluation scores that the researcher must confirm, drafts a report of AI-assisted methods, and keeps separate research projects. It takes particular care with languages and knowledge traditions that are under-represented in AI training data. Runs entirely in the browser: no AI model is called and no user files are uploaded.
