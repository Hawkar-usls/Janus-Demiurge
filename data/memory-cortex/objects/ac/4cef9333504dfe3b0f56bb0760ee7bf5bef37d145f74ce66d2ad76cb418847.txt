Zenodo research description This eighth Classic 777 family examines neurovascular cooperation, apparent barrier permeability, pericyte coverage, archived endothelial-neural signaling, area-normalized leakage, interstitial solute clearance, water balance, diffusion tensors, inert compliance and independent tracer prediction validation. Ten books develop 100 subtopics through 300 candidate equation entries, 82 chapters and 200 Research Gates per book. These are prospective extensions of the master blueprint, not experimental findings. Joint spatial labels, low tracer leakage or a favorable imaging statistic cannot establish neural repair. Each descriptor has an independent reference and a concrete rival, and transfer requires a qualified fluid and tracer ledger. The five-stage inquiry proceeds from physical origin to mathematical evolution, rival counterfactuals, extreme offline extrapolation, and evidence convergence; a sixth page in each chapter preserves independent research inheritance. Neurovascular geometry, tracer and fluid balance, and diffusion-tensor descriptors are kept separate from functional reconnection. The candidate relations are standard or conditional analytical tools, not new validated laws. Transport descriptors use a fixed control volume and source ledger; imaging comparisons require independent calibration. No human intervention, animal intervention, or live biological manipulation is proposed in this working edition.
