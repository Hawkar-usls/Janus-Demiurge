CRE, RCM and HPA reference fronts: Pareto front approximations, Pareto set samples and indicator metadata

Reference front approximations for 105 constrained multi-objective benchmark problems: the eight problems of the CRE suite, 49 of the 50 problems of the real-world constrained suite of the CEC 2021 competition (RCM01–RCM50 without RCM17) and 48 constrained problems of the human-powered aircraft design benchmark HPA. For each problem, the record gives its reference front, a sample of up to 100 points of the front with their decision variables, the union of all the feasible non-dominated solutions found, a sample of random solutions and a row of metadata: dimensions, ideal and nadir points, normalized hypervolume, stability across seeds, feasible rate, constraint-violation normalization and constraint descriptors. The fronts were computed with VAMOS, and independent implementations of the reference code of each suite re-evaluate every sample point. README.md describes every file; the folders are zip archives that unpack into the files listed in MANIFEST.sha256.
