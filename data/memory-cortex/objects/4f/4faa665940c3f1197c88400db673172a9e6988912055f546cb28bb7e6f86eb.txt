Supplementary material (Files S1–S10) for "Bridging Optimization, Food Security, and Sustainability in Food Commodity Supply Chains: A Systematic Review of Methods, Trends, and Critical Research Gaps"

Supplementary Files S1–S10 for the systematic review "Bridging Optimization, Food Security, and Sustainability in Food Commodity Supply Chains: A Systematic Review of Methods, Trends, and Critical Research Gaps" (manuscript JARIE-2605-2014, Journal of Applied Research on Industrial Engineering, revision R3). The files hold the record-level evidence of every stage of the review, so that each PRISMA 2020 transition, quality decision and coded value can be checked and recomputed. Review in brief. Scopus, Web of Science Core Collection, PubMed and Google Scholar (via Publish or Perish) were searched on 29–30 October 2026 for publications from 2000 to 2026. The searches returned 1,456 records; a script removed 579 duplicates, leaving 877 records for title/abstract screening. Of 471 reports sought, 449 were assessed in full text, 367 were assessed for quality, and 130 studies were included. Files - S1 : Protocol Search Exports.zip: review protocol (Parsifal export), search strings (Table S1), search record, search exports - S2 : PRISMA 2020 Checklist.docx: PRISMA 2020 and PRISMA 2020 for Abstracts checklists with manuscript locations. - S3 : Deduplication Log : all 1,456 records with source, Record_ID, DOI, duplicate group, linking rule and retained record; manual checks; comparison with Parsifal. - S3 : Deduplication Scripts : Python deduplication scripts with their inputs and manual decisions. - S4 : PRISMA_Reconciliation per Database : every box of the PRISMA flow diagram recomputed per database - S5: TitleAbstract Screening Log: 877 records with decision, exclusion code and reason. - S6 : FullText Eligibility Log : 471 reports with retrieval status, eligibility decision, exclusion code, reason and page location. - S7 : Geographic Analysis : region definitions, country and region of all 130 studies, regional covariates, distribution tests, correlations and research-capacity analysis. - S7: Capacity_and Selection Calculations : research-capacity baseline and selection decomposition, with bootstrap and permutation draws. - S7 Input Data : input tables of the geographic analysis (SOFI 2024 food insecurity, HDI 2022, case countries, affiliations, QA ratings). - S7 Country_Region_Analysis.ipynb and S7 Country_Region_Analysis_from_input.ipynb: Google Colab notebooks; - S8 : Data Extraction: codebook, study register, data extraction, coding matrix, trade-off coding, keyword thesaurus and VOSviewer files. - S9 : Quality_Assessment : rubric, method guidance, scoring rules , item-level ratings and decisions for 367 reports - S10: Risk of Bias : decision rules, domain ratings D1–D4 with reasons, overall-judgment trace and sensitivity analysis for the 130 included studies. Reproducing the analyses. The deduplication can be rerun with S3_Deduplication_Scripts.zip on the exports in S1. The geographic analysis can be rerun in Google Colab with S7_Country_Region_Analysis_from_input.ipynb and S7_Input_Data.xlsx.
