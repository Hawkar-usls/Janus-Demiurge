REVERSE Final Conference | ePoster Book

This e-poster book brings together all 34 scientific posters presented at the REVERSE Final Conference ( "Antimicrobial Resistance – Results from the REVERSE Approach and the Road Ahead" ), held in a hybrid format at University Hospital Zurich, Switzerland, on 20–21 November 2026 . All 34 posters were displayed electronically throughout the conference venue, and lead authors were invited to pitch their work during dedicated e-poster flash sessions —providing concise, high-impact overviews before in-depth discussions with conference participants. The collection spans work packages, hospital cohorts, infection prevention and control (IPC), antibiotic stewardship (ABS), implementation strategies, one health, and more from across the REVERSE consortium and broader research community. Conference Details: Event: REVERSE Final Conference Dates: 20–21 November 2026 Location: University Hospital Zurich, Zurich, Switzerland (Hybrid) Contents: 34 e-posters presented via electronic display and e-poster flash pitch sessions Project Website & Programme: https://www.reverseproject.eu/scientific-programme
