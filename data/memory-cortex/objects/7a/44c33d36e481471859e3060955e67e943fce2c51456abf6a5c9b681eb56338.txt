Inverse scattering transform for the $N$-component Gerdjikov-Ivanov equation with nonzero boundary conditions

This paper investigates the inverse scattering theory of the $N$-component Gerdjikov-Ivanov equation with nonzero boundary conditions. The analyticity of the Jost eigenfunctions is analyzed, and auxiliary eigenfunctions are constructed using tensor products and generalized cross products, thereby yielding a set of analytic eigenfunctions required to formulate the inverse problem. Three fundamental symmetry relations satisfied by the eigenfunctions and scattering data are established, and the asymptotic behavior of both is examined. We provide a complete characterization of the discrete spectrum and derive the corresponding residue conditions by analyzing the ranks of the relevant matrices. The inverse problem is then formulated as a matrix Riemann-Hilbert problem whose solution is used to reconstruct the potential. In the reflectionless case, an explicit solution formula is obtained. Finally, taking the four-component case as an example, we illustrate the structural features of solitons, breathers, and their interactions.
