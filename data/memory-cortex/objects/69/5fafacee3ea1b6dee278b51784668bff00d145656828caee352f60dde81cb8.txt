Single metrics mislead when evaluating Large Language Models for evidence synthesis and decision-support

Adds search-depth metrics to the retrieval tables. 05_analysis/reframed_metrics.R now records three new values per summary: max_rank_run: the deepest result rank returned (k + offset); - top3_only_run: whether no search went beyond the top three results; never_paged_run: whether no search used an offset.
