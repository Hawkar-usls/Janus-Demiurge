The Construction and Reconstruction of Human Option-Sets

The Construction and Reconstruction of Human Option-Sets proposes that human behavior is best understood not through choices alone, but through the possibility spaces from which those choices emerge. The paper introduces the concept of the option-set , defined as the field of actions, identities, interpretations, and futures that are meaningfully available to an individual at a given moment. Drawing on philosophy, psychology, sociology, developmental theory, ecological psychology, and Relational Structuralism, the paper argues that agency arises within dynamically constructed possibility spaces shaped by perception, object formation, education, social interaction, technology, and culture. The framework is applied to development, expertise, trauma, addiction, law, responsibility, digital systems, and artificial intelligence, offering a unified account of how human possibilities are constructed, expanded, contracted, collapsed, engineered, and reconstructed. The paper proposes a shift in focus from decision-making itself to the formation of the possibility structures that make decisions possible. [The Constr...Option.pdf | PDF]
