Comparative Study of Normal-Behaviour Residual Modeling Methods for SCADA-Based Wind Turbine Anomaly Detection: A Benchmark Study on Why Simple Models Prevail

This repository contains the code for the manuscript "Comparative Study of Normal-Behaviour Residual Modeling Methods for SCADA-Based Wind Turbine Anomaly Detection: A Benchmark Study on Why Simple Models Prevail" . All experiments use fixed random seeds (42). The numbers and configurations in the paper can be reproduced with the scripts below. Each script corresponds to a specific section/table/figure of the manuscript. For further details, please refer to the file named "readme.md".
