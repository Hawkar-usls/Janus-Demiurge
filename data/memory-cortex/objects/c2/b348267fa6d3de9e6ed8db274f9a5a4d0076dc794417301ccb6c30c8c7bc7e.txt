Code, results and data for: Do rumen microbiome markers of feed efficiency and methane transfer between cattle herds? A five-cohort test of published claims

Companion deposit to the manuscript "Do rumen microbiome markers of feed efficiency and methane transfer between cattle herds? A five-cohort test of published claims", prepared for submission to Microbiome (November 2026). This record is the version of the analysis that the manuscript reports: git tag v1.0.5-submission of https://github.com/RushiLagad/rumen-multicohort-replication (commit bc435554). This version: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24544485. Cite all versions with the concept DOI 10.5281/zenodo.24170173. Changes from version v1.0.4-submission (10.5281/zenodo.24378071), which said that the replication labels use normal-theory p values: the labels use the Student-t p of the rescaled ANCOM-BC2 W on the residual df for a target in one cohort and a REML random-effects z test on Fisher z for a target in two or more cohorts. The Student-t p is now reported beside the empirical permutation p for every target estimate (script 146, results/late_p_compare/ , results/manifest/draft21_numbers.tsv , Additional file 2), and README.md, docs/DEVIATIONS.md and docs/ANALYSIS_CLASSES.md are corrected. Also: a note on the two Benjamini-Hochberg families behind Table 2 and the Amendment 48 filtered null; the README's description of when the detection checks were run; the Amendment 46 and 47 review wording in Additional file 2 marked as historical; journal style in the figures ( Succinivibrionaceae in italic in Figure 1, "16S rRNA gene" in Figure 3, a small spacing change in Figure 2); a sample map from every deposit run to its BioSample, animal and count-table column ( results/sample_map/ , script 148); and the reproduction check rerun on this version ( docs/REPRODUCTION_CHECK.md , PASS). The manuscript title is now "Do rumen microbiome markers of feed efficiency and methane transfer between cattle herds? A five-cohort test of published claims". No analysis, number, label or count changed (0 replicated). The repository archive is new; the other four archives are identical to versions v1.0.2 to v1.0.4.
