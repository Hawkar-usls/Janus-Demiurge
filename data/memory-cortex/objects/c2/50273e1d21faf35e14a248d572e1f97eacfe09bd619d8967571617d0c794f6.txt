ENHANCING THE OPERATIONAL RELIABILITY OF FREIGHT CARS BASED ON THE ESTIMATION OF THE FAILURE FLOW PARAMETER

Drawing upon current uncoupling repair (CUR) records for a fleet of 6,222 open-top gondola cars operated by JSC "Temiryo'lkargo" throughout 2025, a quantitative evaluation of the operational dependability (failure-free performance) of a freight gondola car within the inter-repair interval was conducted. The failure flow parameter, mean operating distance between failures, and probability of uncoupling-free operation as a function of mileage were established; the failure stream was disaggregated across major structural assemblies; and the sensitivity of reliability forecasts to the assumed operating time distribution law was examined. The findings demonstrate that the predominant source of uncertainty stems not from the sample size of recorded failures, but rather from the precision of mileage accounting. Moreover, the statutory inter-repair mileage limit of 160 thousand km proves virtually unattainable under prevailing fleet utilization intensity, rendering the 40–60 thousand km range practically pertinent. The boundaries of applicability for the exponential model were delineated, and the structural assemblies contributing most substantially to the overall failure flow were prioritized.
