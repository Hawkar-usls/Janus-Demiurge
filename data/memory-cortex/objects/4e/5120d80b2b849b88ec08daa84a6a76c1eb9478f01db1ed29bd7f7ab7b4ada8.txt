Structural Theorems for Parameter Families of Algebraic Series Reversions

We establish and assemble explicit algebraic, arithmetic and analytic properties of parameterized compositional inverses. We also resolve the recorded binomial-sum conjecture for A063019 and establish the exact forms of its other recorded mathematical statements. The results include a two-step effective recurrence for a cubic inverse family, Jacobi and Gegenbauer parameter-root descriptions, exact modular-Dyck first negative reversals, prime-index congruences, finite-field parity laws, Frobenius divisibility for Hankel matrices, infinite sign changes, an exact asymptotic sign-density law, strict parameter-index total positivity of order two, and boundaries for moment positivity. The proofs are carried out at the stated parameter and index ranges, using formal Lagrange inversion, finite-field algebra, orthogonal polynomials, and singularity analysis. A correspondence table for 46 named sequences and exact worked examples make the parameter specializations and the available finite verification independently reproducible. Classical inversion techniques, published single-sequence formulas, and earlier orthogonal-polynomial and moment results retain their prior attribution.
