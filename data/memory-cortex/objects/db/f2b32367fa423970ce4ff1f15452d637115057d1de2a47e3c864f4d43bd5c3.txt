An invertible counterexample to Peng's zero-dimensional shadowing-extension question

Peng's Question 5.1 in Section 5.1, page 21 of arXiv:2606.14435v3 has a negative answer for compact Hausdorff systems: an open-map obstruction, combined with finite observation languages, yields a system with shadowing that has no zero-dimensional compact Hausdorff shadowing extension. The construction pulls an open surjection over a convergent sequence back to the binary full shift and adds a full-shift component. Marked finite graph languages force any putative extension to give an impossible open zero-dimensional resolution of that surjection. A countable tower and its natural extension give a homeomorphism with the same obstruction, even when the proposed extension is allowed to be noninvertible. Time is one-sided and includes zero.
