Replication package for "Gold beneath the Forest: Gold Prices, Pre-existing Mining Exposure, and Mining-Related Deforestation in the Peruvian Amazon, 2001–2025"

District-year panel, Google Earth Engine extraction scripts, estimation code, outputs and documentation reproducing all tables and figures of the article. Prices use the official World Bank Pink Sheet (real 2010 US$); all headline figures are independently reproducible.
