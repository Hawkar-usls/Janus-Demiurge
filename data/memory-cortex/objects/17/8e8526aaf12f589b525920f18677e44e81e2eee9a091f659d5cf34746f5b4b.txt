Supplementary Material for RadarCover: Cost- and Regime-Aware Set Multicover Pruning for Meteorological Radar Image Super-Resolution

This supplementary material accompanies the paper entitled RadarCover: Cost- and Regime-Aware Set Multicover Pruning for Meteorological Radar Image Super-Resolution , accepted for publication at VISAPP 2027. This material complements the methodology and experimental findings presented in the main manuscript and provides additional technical details to support reproducibility.
