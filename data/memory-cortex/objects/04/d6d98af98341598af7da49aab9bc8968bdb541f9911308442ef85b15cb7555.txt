Replication data for: From Passive Flashcards to Embodied Quests: An Adaptive Augmented-Reality Game for Chinese Children with Special Educational Needs

Aggregated and derived replication values reported in the manuscript: baseline characteristics, mixed-model estimates and 95% CIs, moderation subgroup effects, parallel-mediation direct and indirect effects, and study protocol/analysis parameters. Individual-level identifiable records (children's video, utterance logs, session ratings) remain restricted under ethics approval IRB-2025-06 and are available from the corresponding author under a data-access agreement. Reported values are transcribed verbatim; unreported values are marked NR and were not imputed.
