J Tully Independent Researcher Engineer Australia https://orcid.org/0009-0007-5661-7332 26 th November 2026 1 Verdict The proposition is mathematically sound inside its stated restricted groupoids, once the product, contraction and analytic-trace conventions are fixed compatibly. Both finite examples pass independent exact-arithmetic checks. No substantive counterexample or numerical error was found. The positive cubic on the torus is attainable with an explicit consistent convention; it is not convention-independent. The useful positive result is a Serre-pairing Heisenberg construction equipped with an auxiliary cyclic action and a separately retained product–trace cubic. It is not a reconstruction of geometry, a mirror theorem, a geometric realisation of the auxiliary action, or a deformation-functor comparison.
