The University We Owe Our Nation

The University We Owe Our Nation (record 23118377) This policy handbook sets out practical steps for bringing the Nigerian public university back into line with the purpose for which the nation founded it. In ten chapters, across two volumes, it addresses how knowledge is taught and examined, how leaders form sound judgement, when reforms should be introduced, and how universities should govern the use of artificial intelligence. Each chapter ends with a one-page Regulatory Action Card, naming the problem, the responsible body, the action proposed, a measurable target and a timeline. The proposals are addressed to the National Universities Commission, the Federal Ministry of Education, governing councils and senates, and they are offered for testing and validation, not as settled national standards. The philosophical argument behind the book is set out in its companion volume, The Leadership Compass for Nigerian Higher Education Print edition: Glit Publishers, Kaduna, 2026.
