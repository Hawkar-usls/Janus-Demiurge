Being, Becoming and Beyond Classroom: International Conference Book of Abstracts

Being • Becoming • Beyond the Classroom The title of the conference proposes a sequence and a challenge. Being asks who our students are before formal education begins: their languages, memories, families, social relationships, intellectual curiosity and sense of dignity. Becoming asks what education makes possible: learning, confidence, judgment, competence, agency and the courage to imagine alternative futures. Beyond the Classroom asks where those capacities will be exercised. The answer must include the home, the village, the market, the workplace, public institutions and the natural environment. The Santal Parganas region is an especially meaningful setting for this conversation. The six districts of Dumka, Deoghar, Godda, Jamtara, Pakur and Sahibganj are home to many communities and languages, including strong Santal traditions of land, social life, artistic expression and collective memory. Young people in the region encounter the opportunities of higher education alongside economic and geographical obstacles: variable transport access, family responsibilities, limited availability of some courses, digital connectivity and language differences. A regional conference therefore cannot define educational progress only in terms of examination results or numbers enrolled. It must ask whether students can remain in education, learn well, exercise genuine choice and carry their skills into forms of work and citizenship that they themselves value. A degree is significant, but its meaning is diminished if acquiring it requires a student to abandon all confidence in the community from which they come. The conference’s central proposition Education becomes socially transformative when it enlarges human capability while respecting cultural inheritance, promoting critical inquiry and strengthening responsibility for other persons and the environment. The papers in this book test that proposition in different ways, from Indigenous knowledge and climate justice to AI, conflict prevention, women’s participation, entrepreneurship, educational continuity and language learning
