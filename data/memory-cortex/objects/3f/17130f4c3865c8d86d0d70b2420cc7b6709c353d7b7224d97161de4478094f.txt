Galois Shadows in the Spectrum of Quadratic Tensor Contractions: A Note on Associativity Indices and Exact Arithmetic over ℚ(φ)

Galois Shadows in the Spectrum of Quadratic Tensor Contractions: A Note on Associativity Indices and Exact Arithmetic over ℚ(φ) C. J Tully Independent Researcher Engineer TTG-41/42 Cryptosystems · Australia 23 November 2026 Abstract This note records a precise adjustment to a quasi-associativity index (QEI) together with an independent spectral observation concerning the quadratic contraction of a 52-component tensor. The revised index QEI′ is bounded in [0, 1] by the triangle inequality and differs from the original only by a constant rescaling. The spectral result shows that the contraction decomposes into a pair of conjugate eigenvalues in ℚ(√5), each of multiplicity two, whose sum recovers a previously noted integer invariant. The appearance of the Galois conjugate pair inside the tensor’s own spectrum is recorded and verified. Both contributions are acknowledged.
