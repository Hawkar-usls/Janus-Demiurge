Conioscypha obovoideoispora L. Shao & J. Ma 2026, sp. nov.

Conioscypha obovoideoispora L. Shao & J. Ma sp. nov. Fig. 3 Etymology. “ obovoideoispora ”, referring to the obovoid shape of the conidia. Holotype. GZAAS 23-0532 Description. Saprobic on unidentified decaying wood. Sexual morph: Undetermined. Asexual morph : Dematiaceous Hyphomycetous. Colonies on natural substrate effuse, black, gregarious, glistening. Mycelium semi-immersed, composed of hyaline to subhyaline, branched, septate, smooth hyphae. Conidiophores micronematous, mononematous, arising directly from the hyphae, and reduced to conidiogenous cells, hyaline. Conidiogenous cells phialidic, integrated, sessile or on micronematous conidiophores, with cup-shaped multi-collarette, hyaline, smooth. Conidia 12–18 µm × 12.5–18 µm (x ̄ = 15 × 14.4 µm, n = 30), acrogenous, solitary, globose to subglobose, obovoid, reddish brown to brown, aseptate, guttulate, smooth, truncate at base with a circular basal scar. Culture characteristics. Conidia germinating on PDA within 24 h, producing germ tubes from the basal cell. Colonies on PDA irregular to subcircular, dense, raised, fluffy, with undulate margins; surface velvety to finely floccose, distinctly zonate. Centre dark olivaceous-grey to greyish black, surrounded by a pale-yellow intermediate zone and a white to cream-white marginal fringe. Exudates and diffusible pigments absent. Reverse pale yellow to yellowish cream, olivaceous-grey to black at the center, becoming paler towards the margin; margin cream-white, crenate to slightly lobate. Diffusible pigments absent. Material examined. Saprobic on unidentified decaying branches, Liupanshui City, Guizhou Province, China, 29 th March 2023, Lei Shao & Jian Ma, LS 158 (GZAAS 23-0532, holotype), ex-type living culture GZCC 23-0527; LS 126 (GZAAS 23-0533, paratype), living culture GZCC 23-0528. Note. Our phylogenetic analysis (Fig. 1) shows that the newly isolated strains cluster with Conioscypha hydei (GZCC 24-0284) with robust support (99 % ML / 0.99 BYPP). Morphologically, our collection differs from C. hydei by its smaller conidia (12–18 μm vs. up to 26 μm) that are obovoid and guttulate, contrasting with the globose to subglobose conidia of C. hydei (Xiao et al. 2025). Sequence comparisons between our collection and the ex-type strain of C. hydei revealed significant nucleotide differences: 2.1 % in ITS (11 / 535 bp), 0.25 % in LSU (2 / 803 bp), 1.1 % in SSU (11 / 980 bp), and 4.3 % in RPB 2 (45 / 1,040 bp), excluding gaps. Based on both morphological distinctions and multigene phylogenetic evidence, our collection represents a novel species, herein introduced as C. obovoideoispora following the recommendations of Chethana et al. (2021). The two strains obtained in this study (GZCC 23-0527 and GZCC 23-0528) are morphologically identical. Pairwise sequence alignment between these two strains revealed minimal intra-specific variations, including 1 % in ITS (6 / 579 bp), 0.26 % in LSU (2 / 778 bp), and 0.4 % in SSU (2 / 488 bp excluding gaps). Therefore, both strains are accommodated within the same species and described here as C. obovoideoispora .
