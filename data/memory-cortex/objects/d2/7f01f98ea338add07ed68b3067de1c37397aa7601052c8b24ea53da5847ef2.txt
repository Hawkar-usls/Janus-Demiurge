Reproducibility deposit for "Evidence Adjudication and Inferential Uncertainty in Research Synthesis: An Empirical Benchmark and Monte Carlo Evaluation"

This repository contains the data, analytical code, simulation specifications, and computational results supporting the manuscript entitled Evidence Adjudication and Inferential Uncertainty in Research Synthesis: An Empirical Benchmark and Monte Carlo Evaluation . The materials include an empirical corpus of 356 measurement representations from 243 studies, coding documentation, bibliographic study identifiers, Python scripts for the empirical benchmark and Monte Carlo evaluation, complementary analyses, and instructions for reproducing the reported results. The repository also includes software requirements, random seeds, and file checksums. Documentation specifies the scope and limitations of the available corpus records. This deposit supports the computational reproducibility and methodological transparency of the research.
