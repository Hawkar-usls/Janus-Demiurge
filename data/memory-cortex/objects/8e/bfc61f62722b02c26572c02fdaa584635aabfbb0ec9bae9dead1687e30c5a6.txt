Exact O(N) Spectral Projection on J(42, 2), Nonlinear Optical Metric Engineering, and Continuous Burning-Plasma Magnetohydrodynamic Containment

Building on two companion studies of Heegner-rigid vorton manifolds and seventy-two-fold incommensurability on the 861-node Johnson graph, this paper connects finite spectral admissibility to three concrete domains of mathematical physics: ill-posed inverse problems, nonlinear optical metric engineering, and continuous burning-plasma magnetohydrodynamics . First, exploiting the sparse binary incidence structure of the 861-node graph yields four exact, division-free integer energy identities across all three spectral bands and cuts orthogonal projection from more than seven hundred forty thousand dense operations to roughly five thousand simple steps with zero matrix storage . This establishes provable convergence and noise bounds for severely underdetermined inverse problems alongside uniform error control for Chebyshev polynomial operator layers . Second, in nonlinear optics, the paper verifies the exact Gordon–Kerr optical metric in moving vortical dielectrics, derives the Fresnel-drag topological phase shift induced by vorton circulation, and proves an all-order nonlinear invariance theorem: all thirty-six highest-order seventy-two-fold and thirty-six-fold harmonic subspaces remain strictly confined to the high-energy gap band under arbitrary local Kerr nonlinearities at any optical intensity, with zero nonlinear leakage into the admissible subspace . Third, in magnetized plasma physics, the paper proves exact Hall-term cancellations and generalized ion-helicity conservation, solves exact strained flux-rope and reversing current-sheet equilibria showing why fixed-flux magnetic tubes inevitably force reconnection at high conductivity, and links spectral-gap suppression of edge turbulence directly to classical deuterium–tritium ignition thresholds and thermal burn stability .
