HiMIC-Daily VPD: Daily 1 km Vapor Pressure Deficit over China, 2003–2025

This record provides the Vapor Pressure Deficit (VPD) component of the Hi gh-resolution ( d aily and 1 km) atmospheric M oisture I ndex C ollection over China (HiMIC-Daily) . VPD is a key indicator of moisture stress on vegetation and fire risk, and complements the other five moisture indicators in the collection. The dataset was generated using the Light Gradient Boosting Machine (LightGBM) algorithm for spatial reconstruction, integrating meteorological station observations with multiple high-resolution datasets, including land surface temperature (MOD11A1/MYD11A1), land cover (MCD12Q1), topographic variables (MERIT DEM), column water vapor (MCD19A2), vapor pressure (TerraClimate), 2-m temperature and dew point temperature (ERA5-Land), and population density (WorldPop). LightGBM was selected for its higher efficiency and robustness compared to other tree-based algorithms. For VPD, the reconstruction achieves an R ² of 0.906, MAE of 0.983 hPa, and RMSE of 1.411 hPa. The product has a spatial resolution of 1 km × 1 km and a daily temporal resolution, covering China from January 1, 2003, to December 31, 2025. The dataset is organized into annual NetCDF files, one file per year, with daily data indexed by the time coordinate. To optimize storage and maintain precision, values are scaled by a factor of 100 and stored as 16-bit integer (int16); original values are restored by dividing the stored data by 100. The dataset is referenced to the World Geodetic System (WGS) 1984 Coordinate System. Comprehensive information on file naming and other specifications is documented in the "README.pdf". The HiMIC-Daily dataset is organized into annual NetCDF files, with one file per indicator per year. Currently, each moisture indicator is provided through its own dedicated download link: RH (2003–2020) AVP (2003–2020) VPD (2003–2020) DPT (2003–2020) MR (2003–2020) SH (2003–2020) Because the dataset is updated annually, one additional link will be added each year as a new annual file becomes available: 2021 (RH, AVP, VPD, DPT, MR, SH) 2022 (RH, AVP, VPD, DPT, MR, SH) 2023 (RH, AVP, VPD, DPT, MR, SH) 2024 (RH, AVP, VPD, DPT, MR, SH) 2025 (RH, AVP, VPD, DPT, MR, SH) More data will be added in the future.
