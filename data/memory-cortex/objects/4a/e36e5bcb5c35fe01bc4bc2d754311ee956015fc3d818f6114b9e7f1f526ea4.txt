StepUp Core

StepUp is a dynamic build tool for workflows written in Python. Like Make, it reruns only the steps whose inputs have changed. Unlike most build tools, StepUp starts executing steps with only partial knowledge of the workflow. A platform-independent plan.py script defines the initial steps, and while the workflow runs, any step can add more steps and dependencies, based on the outputs built so far. Workflows can thus grow with your work, without having to fit your ideas into a rigid framework. StepUp is therefore ideal for workflows that are less structured than software builds, such as those that produce scientific publications or datasets.
