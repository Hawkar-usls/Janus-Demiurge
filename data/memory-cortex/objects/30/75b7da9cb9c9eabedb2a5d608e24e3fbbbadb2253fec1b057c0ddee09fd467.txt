DFU-VCell reproducibility package: fibroblast matrix signatures and CRISPRa prioritization in diabetic foot ulcer research (round-1 revision)

Reanalysis code, processed outputs and harmonized result tables underlying the revised manuscript 'Fibroblast Matrix Signatures in Human Wounds and CRISPRa-Based Prioritization of EGR3 as a Candidate Regulator for Diabetic Foot Ulcer Research'. Includes exact two-sided permutation tests, ACTA2/TAGLN ablation panel series, recomputed CRISPRa rankings on three axis definitions, and the MASTER_numbers.json single source of all reported values. Mirrors GitHub release v1.2.1-revision (github.com/rht888666/dfu-vcell-reproducibility).
