AffectFusion: A Multilingual Multimodal Text-and-Emoji Emotion Recognition Dataset (English, Bangla, and Banglish)

AffectFusion v1.0.0 is an open, PII-anonymized, zero-leakage multilingual multimodal benchmark dataset for text-and-emoji emotion recognition spanning English (ENG), Native Bengali (BN), and Romanized Banglish (BNG) across 9 fine-grained emotion classes: Joy, Neutral, Anger, Sadness, Surprise, Sarcasm, Fear, Disgust, and Anticipation. Key Dataset Properties: • Total Size: 30,000 dual-stream samples partitioned strictly 80/10/10 into train_24k (24,000 balanced rows; 2,666–2,667 per class), val_3k (3,000 100% real scraped rows), and test_3k (3,000 100% real scraped rows). • Zero-Leakage Guarantee: Deduplicated by normalized text MD5 hash across splits; strictly 0 augmented rows and 0 external dataset rows appear in val_3k or test_3k. • 5,000 Blinded Human-Verified Gold Annotations: Independently verified by two researchers with AI labels hidden (2,500 rows each), achieving 83.00% raw agreement and Cohen's κ = 0.8087 (ENG: 0.8129, BN: 0.8128, BNG: 0.7958). Upgrades test_3k to 100% human-verified gold (574 AI labels corrected) and val_3k to 66.7% gold (276 corrected). • Unified 25-Column Dual-Stream Schema: Provides separated text_stream, emoji_stream, Unicode CLDR demoji_text, and baseline_input alongside emoji density, punctuation cues, sarcasm emoji flags, and transparent provenance flags (is_augmented, is_external_train_only, is_human_verified). • Privacy-by-Design: Automated regex masking replaces all URLs (<URL>), user handles (<USER>), emails (<EMAIL>), and phone numbers (<PHONE>) prior to storage. • Reference Phase-I Benchmarks on 100% Gold test_3k (N = 3,000): CNN-BiLSTM (54.20% Acc, 0.5375 Macro-F1), XLM-R Text-Only (54.50% Acc, 0.5341 Macro-F1), and XLM-R Early Fusion (64.07% Acc, 0.6366 Macro-F1; +13.17% Macro-F1 gain on emoji-bearing posts, paired t = 11.24, McNemar χ² = 117.86, p < 0.0001). Companion source code, Colab notebooks, and evaluation scripts are available at: https://github.com/ragibcs/AffectFusion
