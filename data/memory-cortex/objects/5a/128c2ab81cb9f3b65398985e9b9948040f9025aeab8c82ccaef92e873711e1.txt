Supplementary Materials for a PhD Thesis on LLM-Assisted University Export Control Screening

These supplementary materials accompany a PhD thesis investigating the use of large language models (LLMs) for automated export-control compliance screening in UK universities. The research examines LLM-based classification, regulatory information retrieval, explainability, and the evaluation of compliance-support systems. The record contains two supplementary documents: Supplementary Material S1: Fine-Tuning Methodology and Supporting Analyses S1 provides additional methodological documentation, including fine-tuning configurations, implementation details, worked examples, and supporting reference material for the export-control screening system. Supplementary Material S2: ECIR Retrieval Evaluation Data and Reproducibility S2 documents the evaluation of regulatory information retrieval, including the relevance-judgement protocol, retrieval configurations, evaluation measures, aggregate results, and methodological limitations. Together, these documents provide additional information supporting the methods and findings presented in the doctoral thesis. They are intended to facilitate transparency, scholarly scrutiny, and reproducibility. Data availability: This record contains two supplementary PDF documents and an accompanying ZIP archive of supporting ECIR retrieval-evaluation materials. The archive includes evaluation data, relevance judgements, methodological documentation, and supporting analysis files. The export control officer has authorised the public release of her individual relevance judgements under the applicable licence.
