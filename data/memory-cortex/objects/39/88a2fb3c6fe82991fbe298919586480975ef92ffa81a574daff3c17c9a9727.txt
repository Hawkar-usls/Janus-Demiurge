Replication-support data for Navigating History Through Play: Designing an AR-Enhanced Board Game for the Chinese Maritime Silk Road with Emergent Narrative Mechanisms

Aggregated/derived replication-support data for the study: participant demographic aggregates, Table 1 primary outcome measures (AR, tablet, traditional conditions), Table 2 AR content interaction statistics, narrative moment counts, and the study protocol. Values are transcribed verbatim from the manuscript; unreported values are marked NR and were not imputed. Individual-level recordings, transcripts, session logs, and ratings remain restricted under ethics and privacy conditions and are available from the corresponding author on reasonable request, subject to ethics approval.
