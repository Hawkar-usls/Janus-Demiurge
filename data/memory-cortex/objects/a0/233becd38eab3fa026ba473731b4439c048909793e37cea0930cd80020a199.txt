Zenodo research description This ninth Classic 777 family investigates peripheral nerve gap bridging, Schwann-cell alignment, myelin geometry and conduction, sensory target mapping, archived conduit versus autograft comparisons, inert spinal cavity geometry, long-distance path tracing, lesion cue overlap, spared-circuit attribution and archived motor-signal timing. The ten books develop 100 subtopics through 300 candidate equation entries, 82 chapters and 200 Research Gates per book. The working edition is prospective rather than experimental. A longer trace, reduced cavity void or a detectable signal cannot establish functional neural regeneration. Independent path history and strong matched comparisons are essential, and unresolved evidence remains explicit. The five-stage inquiry proceeds from physical origin to mathematical evolution, rival counterfactuals, extreme offline extrapolation, and evidence convergence; a sixth page in each chapter preserves independent research inheritance. Path geometry, myelin descriptors, lesion masks, and timing statistics are kept separate from functional reconnection. The candidate relations are standard or conditional analytical tools, not new validated laws. Path descriptors require registered historical identity; waveform comparisons require independent timing and artifact references. No human intervention, animal intervention, or live biological manipulation is proposed in this working edition.
