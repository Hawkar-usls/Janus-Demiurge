Leakage-Controlled Active-Learning Benchmark Dataset and Code

This is a leakage-controlled active-learning benchmark archive evaluating diversity-, uncertainty-, committee-, Gaussian-process-, and D-optimal acquisition policies on molecular adsorption free-energy prediction under cold- and warm-start replay regimes. The release includes identity-level learning-curve metrics, family-locked inferential comparisons, operational threshold sensitivity analyses, machine-readable figure datasets, formatted reference tables, and reproducible Python verification scripts tied to protocol SHA-256 e3729ec2ec9d1df5d6e3f57121e4596a8e33454125d5210e2a6631e591f2f059. Raw upstream descriptor matrices and adsorption free-energy tables from an ongoing companion study are withheld pending publication, with their SHA-256 provenance digests retained in data/protocol_config.json.
