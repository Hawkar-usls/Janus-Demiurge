NWNet: orthology inference by small-world sparsification of all-against-all comparison

Supplementary data for "NWNet: orthology inference by small-world sparsification of all-against-all comparison" This archive contains the genome lists, sampling scripts and benchmark results used to prepare and evaluate the benchmarks in the NWNet manuscript (Lu, Zuo & Zhi). Contents ecoli/ — Escherichia coli genome acquisition and sampling: the 12,308 non-redundant, quality-filtered genome accessions, the 1,000-genome ANI-stratified sample, the 100–500 genome benchmark subsets, the fastANI results, and the sampling scripts. qfo/ — Quest for Orthologs 2022 benchmark: the local reference-phylogeny scoring scripts and the per-configuration SwissTree and TreeFam-A TPR/PPV scores for the nine evaluated configurations. Data sources The 404,453 E. coli genomes were downloaded from the NCBI Assembly database on 17 June 2026. The QfO 2022 reference data are available from OpenEBench ( https://openebench.bsc.es ); the 78 species proteomes (988,778 proteins) are available from UniProt. Neither is re-packaged here. The NWNet source code is available at https://github.com/MSE-Lab/NWNet under GPL-3.0.
