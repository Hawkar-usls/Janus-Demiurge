Analysis code for Antagonistic pleiotropy at CTRB1/CTRB2 and shared polygenic architecture across pancreatic cancer, pancreatitis, and type 2 diabetes

Versioned analysis-code and statistical-upgrade archive ( v3 , 24 November 2026) supporting Antagonistic pleiotropy at CTRB1/CTRB2 and shared polygenic architecture across pancreatic cancer, pancreatitis, and type 2 diabetes . Supersedes published v2 ( 10.5281/zenodo.24458358 ) and v1 ( 10.5281/zenodo.24339916 ) under Zenodo concept DOI 10.5281/zenodo.24339915 . New in v3: Upgraded 65-sheet supplementary workbook ( Additional_file_2_Tables_integrated.xlsx ; 55 cleaned original tables bit-for-bit preserved plus new tables S16–S25). Disjoint two-split cross-cohort MR meta-analysis, formal Fisher- z Steiger selection-bias audit, FinnGen R13 histology-restricted ductal PDAC leave- CTRB1/CTRB2 -out MR sensitivity, and locus-stratified CTRB1/CTRB2 and ABO pleiotropy decompositions (S16–S18). LDSC cross-trait intercept co-coding inversion and harmonized-scale Wray–Lee–Kendler (2012) liability-threshold misclassification grids for both PC–CP co-coding and pancreatogenic T3cDM/paraneoplastic NOD misclassified as T2D (S19–S20). Well-calibrated single-cohort UK Biobank–only T2D LDSC validation (univariate intercept 1.0630; S21). Full-R12 genome-wide PLACO pleiotropy scan (9.22–9.32M SNVs/pair) uncovering concordant NR5A2 (1q32.1) and pervasive antagonistic pleiotropy in metabolic–exocrine pairs, audited with both direct UK Biobank–only T2D PLACO and double-penalized fixed-variance genomic-control stress tests (S22). CAUSE v1.2.0 (4 disjoint splits with Pareto- k diagnostics), HM3 LCV, 72-model SuSiE-RSS ( 1000G_EUR_503 LD–Z mismatch audit), fitted .rds model objects, and East Asian LDSC (Yuan 2026 × BioBank Japan) power audit (S23–S25). Deterministic publication-grade SVG/PDF/PNG generators for Figures 1, 2c, 3, and 5. Original analysis code and documentation are released under the MIT License. All local workstation paths and non-public metadata have been scrubbed and verified against the root MANIFEST_SHA256.tsv . Archive scope: This release contains analysis code, selected derived results, the supplementary workbook, and 76 fitted model objects (72 SuSiE-RSS and 4 CAUSE). It is not a complete raw-data or historical MR/MAGMA-input mirror.
