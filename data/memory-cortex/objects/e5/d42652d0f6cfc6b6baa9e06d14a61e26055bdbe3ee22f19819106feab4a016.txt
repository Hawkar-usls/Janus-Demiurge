RolloutSecBench evidence archive

Evidence archive for: Mohammed, I.; Cho, H.; Shashidhar, N. Model Rollout Evasion: Quantifying and Predicting Evasion Exposure During Progressive Deployment of Machine Learning Detectors (manuscript submitted to Electronics, MDPI). Study summary Machine learning intrusion detectors are updated through progressive rollouts, during which two model revisions serve traffic at the same time. RolloutSecBench serves two frozen XGBoost revisions trained on UNSW-NB15 behind a weighted router on a single-node Kubernetes cluster and records the revision that served every request. Four rollout states (0%, 10%, 50% and 100% of traffic to the newer revision) are crossed with a non-adaptive attacker that submits each malicious sample once or five times, with five valid runs per condition. Although both revisions passed the same acceptance criteria, they disagreed on 149 of 38,263 malicious samples (0.389%). Resubmission increased evasion only while both revisions served, and within individual runs exposure followed the realized routing of the disagreement set rather than the configured weight. Version 1.1 adds the extension analyses of the revised article: duplicate-aware disagreement, alternative tests of the confirmatory decisions, a routing simulator validated on all 40 runs, 72 further revision pairs on UNSW-NB15 and CIC-IDS2017, live runs under session-affinity and content-keyed routing, offline attacker variants and a directional-disagreement gate. Contents rolloutsecbench-evidence-v1.tar.gz (confirmatory campaign; unchanged from version 1): Frozen model artifacts of both detector revisions and the preprocessor Pre-registration and analysis manifests of the confirmatory campaign Append-only run registry: 41 runs executed, 40 valid; the one invalid run was replaced by a pre-registered reserve Request-level client and router evidence for every run The analysis of record ( analysis_20261022T200702Z ), including the six files that supply the values and generated tables of the confirmatory campaign rolloutsecbench-evidence-ext-v1.1.tar.gz (extension analyses; new in version 1.1): Outputs of the extension analyses, with the values and tables of the article that they supply Raw client and router evidence of the 12 live routing-policy runs and 4 qualification runs, with their registry Data and provenance of the revised figures and tables In both archives, samples are identified by identifier only; no feature values of either dataset are included. Not included The UNSW-NB15 dataset, the corrected CIC-IDS2017 release and the split files derived from them are not redistributed. UNSW-NB15 is distributed by its authors (free for academic research; commercial use by agreement with the authors), and the corrected CIC-IDS2017 release by its providers. The released code recreates the UNSW-NB15 splits from the two official files and verifies them against Table S1 of the article. The plan of the extension analyses is not included; its SHA-256 and timestamp proof are listed in Table S4 of the article.
