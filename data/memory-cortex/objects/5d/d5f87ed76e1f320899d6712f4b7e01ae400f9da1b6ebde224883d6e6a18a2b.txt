The ten flagships distinguish axon geometry, neurotrophic factors, myelin geometry, Schwann cell labels, synaptic ratios, spinal network change, sensory motor lag, neuroimmune prediction, digital scaffold deformation and functional discordance. Each retains a separate endpoint and a conventional comparator. Positive predictive utility, if later demonstrated, would not by itself establish a physiological mechanism, independent anatomical channel, therapeutic action, regenerative benefit or quantum advantage. The family is organized as ten independently navigable 777-page draft volumes. Conditional mathematical relations, synthetic numerical grids, five-stage research chapters, future-oriented research gates and candidate reconstruction objects create an evidence architecture. Shared statistical diagnostics deliberately recur as methods rather than being presented as novel laws. The central challenge is to preserve the freedom of subsequent investigators to falsify the candidate or explain an observation through conventional anatomy and physics. The research remains limited to mathematical simulation, digital reference objects and passive or archival designs.
