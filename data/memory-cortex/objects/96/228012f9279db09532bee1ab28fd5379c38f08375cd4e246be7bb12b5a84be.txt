Residual Risk and Contestable Deployment: Post-Quantum Erasure, Authorized-Action Boundaries, and Dual-Core Statecraft in Practice

PalimpsestSec-Bench results, leave-one-out ablations, and institutional Dual-Core deployment dossiers (extending Bangladesh and Estonia analyses) demonstrate measurable reduction in Expected Free Energy paralysis windows and switching costs. The volume supplies the practical baseline for contestable Dual-Core statecraft across the 2026–2035 bifurcation.
