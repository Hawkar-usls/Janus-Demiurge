Zenodo research description This seventh Classic 777 family examines neuroimmune temporal states, debris accounting, synaptic retention, metabolic exchange, heterogeneity, infiltration, archived cytokine-associated morphology, immune-matrix geometry, inert release and noncompensatory clearance retention. Each descriptor has an independent reference and an explicit alternative explanation. Ten books develop 100 subtopics through 300 candidate equation entries, 82 chapters and 200 Research Gates per book. These are prospective extensions of the master blueprint, not experimental findings. A lower inflammatory signal, faster apparent clearance or a changing interface is not sufficient evidence for neural repair. The five-stage inquiry proceeds from physical origin to mathematical evolution, rival counterfactuals, extreme offline extrapolation, and evidence convergence; a sixth page in each chapter preserves independent research inheritance. Immune-state measurements, registered structural descriptors, and source-material mass balances are kept separate from functional reconnection. The candidate relations are standard or conditional analytical tools and are not new validated laws. No human intervention, animal intervention, or live biological manipulation is proposed in this working edition.
