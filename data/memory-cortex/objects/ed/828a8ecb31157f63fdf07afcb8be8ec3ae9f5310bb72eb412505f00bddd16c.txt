SylEmotions: A Dual-Script Sylheti Emotion Dataset (Syloti Nagri and Bangla Script)

SylEmotions is a corpus of 7,051 Sylheti sentences labelled with seven emotions: Angry (1,000), Happy (1,001), Sad (1,000), Hate (1,004), Fear (999), Surprise (1,023) and Neutral (1,024). Each sentence is given in both Syloti Nagri and Bangla script. The file has three columns: syloti_in_bangla_script, syloti and emotion. Exact duplicates were removed. This dataset accompanies the paper "SylEmotions: A Dual-Script Corpus and Benchmark for Emotion Detection in Low-Resource Sylheti", 29th International Conference on Computer and Information Technology (ICCIT 2026).
