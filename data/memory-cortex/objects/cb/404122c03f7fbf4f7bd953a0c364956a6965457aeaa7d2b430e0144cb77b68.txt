The chemical warrant gap: From predictive discovery to reliable chemical knowledge

This Perspective introduces a claim-sensitive framework for evaluating evidential warrant in chemistry as computational modeling, artificial intelligence, and automated experimentation expand discovery workflows . Defining the chemical warrant gap as the mismatch between generating plausible candidates or predictions and the evidential strength required to justify corresponding chemical claims, the paper organizes assessment around five selectively activated dimensions material realization, chemical identity, evidential and measurement adequacy, mechanistic discrimination, and operational robustness and domain transferability alongside cross-cutting provenance and a distinct consideration of safety and sustainability for responsible application . The framework is operationalized through a five-step evaluation procedure and illustrated via a bounded analysis of Pd surface reconstruction as well as applications across molecular generation, materials discovery, and automated laboratories .
