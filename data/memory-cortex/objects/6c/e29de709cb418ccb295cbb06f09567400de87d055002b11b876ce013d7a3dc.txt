What's new Added 🎉 Hu-Sawicki f(R) with hi_class-based codes (frahi_class): the CLASS wrapper is configurable through the class_module and class_object options (default classy.Class ), log10fR0 is translated to parameters_smg for cosmo_model='HuSawicki' , and CLASS profiles frahiclass_hs_photo*.yaml are added. Nonlinear f(R) boosts winther2019 and emantis ( cosmology/modified_gravity.py ): the f(R) code gives the linear spectra and A_s , and the nonlinear spectrum is a LCDM CLASS spectrum with the same A_s times the boost, reproducing the MontePython likelihood euclid_photometric_z_fofr (Sefa76/photometric_fofR); the Winther et al. 2019 fitting formula is a verbatim copy of its MGfit_Winther.py . Documentation page modified_gravity_fR on Hu-Sawicki f(R) forecasts: options, profiles, nonlinear boosts and the frahi_class flags they use. GCspxIM observable for the galaxy x intensity-mapping cross-spectrum. Listing all three of GCsp , GCspxIM and IM (or GCspxIM with the option spectro_joint=True ) runs a joint auto + cross analysis per redshift bin with the full 2x2 Gaussian covariance of the two fields, so the three spectra no longer have to be added by hand (new module cosmicjellyfish.LSSsurvey.spectro_modes , setting spectro_mode ). The joint analysis uses the standard finite-difference derivatives and the overlapping redshift bins. Updated DESI specifications DESI-BGS-Validation2023 , DESI-LRG-Validation2023 , DESI-ELG-Validation2023 and DESI-QSO-Validation2023 (new LRG and quasar samples) from Table 7 and the bias recipe b = A/D(z) of DESI Collaboration, arXiv:2306.06307, also implemented in GoFish (https://github.com/ladosamushia/GoFish). The legacy DESI-BGS-SKAOPaper and DESI-ELG-SKAOPaper files are unchanged. They are generated by scripts/validation/generate_desi_validation_specs.py , available as desi2023-* cases in scripts/validation/run_survey_forecasts.py , plotted in plot_survey_validation.py , and compared species by species against the legacy files and Euclid in notebook 09 and the survey validation docs page. skao-im-desi-joint case in scripts/validation/run_survey_forecasts.py and new tests in tests/spectro_modes_test.py . Survey validation roadmap docs/SURVEY_VALIDATION_ROADMAP.md for the non-Euclid configurations (DESI, Rubin/LSST, SKAO and Planck), including the provenance map that traces each historical input to the current specification schema. Historical -SKAOPaper survey specifications transcribed from the SKAO forecast project of Casas et al. (2023): DESI-BGS-SKAOPaper , DESI-ELG-SKAOPaper , Rubin-Photometric-SKAOPaper , SKAO-Photometric-SKAOPaper , SKAO-Spectroscopic-SKAOPaper and SKAO-IM-Redbook-DESI-ELG . They are opt-in files under other_survey_specifications/ and do not change any existing default. Survey observable validation harness: scripts/validation/survey_observable_reference.py (independent transcription of the historical galaxy-clustering, intensity-mapping and photometric recipes) and scripts/validation/validate_survey_observables.py , which compares the package against it for every migrated configuration and writes a JSON report plus the SKAO Fig. 1 diagnostic. Results are recorded in docs/SURVEY_VALIDATION_ROADMAP.md . Fast guard tests tests/survey_observables_validation_test.py for the reference formulas and the historical Fig. 1 table. Survey forecast comparison harness scripts/validation/run_survey_forecasts.py , which runs Euclid, Rubin/LSST, DESI, SKAO (photometric, HI galaxies, intensity mapping and the IM x DESI-ELG cross-spectrum) and a Planck-like CMB forecast on a common GR fiducial in both LCDM and w0waCDM, reports marginalised errors and dark-energy figures of merit, and writes Markdown/JSON summaries. Results are recorded in docs/SURVEY_VALIDATION_ROADMAP.md . scripts/validation/plot_survey_validation.py and the resulting figures (survey inputs, marginalised-error panels, paired w0 - wa Fisher ellipses and the SKAO Fig. 1 diagnostic), plus the documentation page docs/source/survey_validation.md and the executed tutorial notebooks/09-Survey-validation.ipynb . Changed ⚠️ The observables ["GCsp", "IM"] used to select the cross-spectrum only. They keep doing so, now with a FutureWarning ; use ["GCspxIM"] instead. The survey scripts use the new name. Fig. 4 of the paper compares Euclid and Rubin/LSST photometry with Euclid GCsp, DESI ELG+BGS and SKAO IM + DESI ELG (auto + cross) in separate photometric and spectroscopic w0 - wa panels with explicit axis limits. The survey forecast harness uses a relative finite-difference step of 0.002 for the galaxy-clustering and intensity-mapping probes. At the previous 0.01 their sigma8 errors were under-converged by 20-38%; the photometric probes are unchanged. Fixed ✅ The reference LCDM spectrum used by the f(R) boosts keeps the shared inputs of the resolved f(R) run, including parameters set only in the HuSawicki CLASS section (for example T_cmb ); gravity-specific flags and the scalar-field closure are still excluded. The e-MANTIS boost clips to the overlap of the likelihood and emulator ranges, avoiding an out-of-range error near the lower LCDM sigma8 bound. Review fixes for the cross-spectrum and joint spectroscopic analyses: Fisher output files carry the spectrum mode ( ..._GCspIMGCspxIM_Ig_FM.txt and ..._joint_FM.txt ), because a cross-only and a joint forecast resolve to the same observables and used to overwrite each other. Auto-spectrum and photometric file names are unchanged. load_fisher_from_json replays a cross-only snapshot as cross-only instead of re-resolving its observables as a joint analysis. When the galaxy and intensity-mapping grids differ in extent, every shared bin now takes its volume and galaxy density from its own redshift interval (identical grids are unchanged). Bins that share a midpoint but not their edges are rejected with a clear error, where they used to run with the volume of a different interval. Selecting sp_bias_model: linear_log defaults the bias root to lnb . An explicitly incompatible sp_bias_root raises a clear ValueError before the forecast. The validation scripts reject non-finite per-bin differences instead of recording them as agreement, accept array-valued velocity dispersions, and label the temperature units of the plotted spectra and of the normalised 21 cm noise. The Fisher ellipses of the survey figures use the 68.27% and 95.45% joint levels of fishconsumer . The docs and docs/KNOWN_ISSUES.md state that DESI and Rubin without an explicit specification silently load the Euclid default, and the roadmap no longer presents the cause of the SKAO Fig. 1 amplitude offset or a cross-backend agreement as established. Survey validation review follow-ups: combinations sum the full nuisance-marginalised Fisher blocks (and the harness now refuses to combine a report that lacks them, keeping the summed block for reuse), the IM volume uses fsky_IM and the cross-spectrum marks its nested-footprint default (optional fsky_cross override), the paper ellipses are centred on (w0, wa) = (-1, 0) , the fixed mnu = 0.06 eV is explicit, and the validation claims were narrowed to what is actually checked. The cross-spectrum covariance test now exercises veff_Ig , and the w0 - wa figure is regrouped into four paired panels without the much weaker Planck-like run, with missing inputs raising instead of being silently skipped. Example notebooks 04, 05 and 06 now run top to bottom (reported by Kazuya): notebook 04 defines the Planck parameter list and skips the covariance comparison when its Part-2 inputs are missing, 05 combines Fishers with matching fiducials instead of failing on the Planck-like ones produced by 03, 06 passes the Fisher result (not the FisherMatrix object) to the nuisance prior helper and writes a commented chain header, and 03 writes one Fisher file per survey. The kernel metadata of 04 and 07 is the generic python3 . Notebook 04 also selects a single producing package version instead of the lexicographically last Fisher file, and checks the exact Planck .covmat and .likestats files before running its optional diagnostics. fisher_operations.information_gain no longer fails: it built an all-zero Fisher matrix, which the fisher_matrix constructor reduces to zero parameters. The prior argument is now optional, and a prior may supply a parameter that is absent from the second Fisher matrix: the statistical term zero-pads it instead of asking reshuffle for a parameter that is not there. scripts/planck/run_planck_diagnostics_suite.sh builds its Fisher filenames from the package VERSION instead of the hard-coded v1.3.0 ; the combine step previously read names the producers no longer write and stopped before creating the combined Fisher.
