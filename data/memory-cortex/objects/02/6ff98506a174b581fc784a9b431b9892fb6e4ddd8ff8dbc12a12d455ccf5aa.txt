Data for the article Ray tracing a heritage survey with support-constrained depth super-resolution on 48 GPUs

A laser survey can contain millions of points yet leave visible gaps around architectural relief. We reconstructed local depth fields from 86.6 million survey points at the Sanctuary of Bom Jesus de Matosinhos in Congonhas, Brazil, and ray traced them directly on 48 workstation GPUs. Two nested resolutions represent 232.8 million and 5.82 billion supported surface sites. On a façade region rebuilt after withholding 20% of its measurements, refinement reduced median absolute depth error by 8.9% relative to the coarser reconstruction, while increasing the 95th percentile by 3.2%. For a fixed 4K view, median time per single-sample pass decreased from 397 to 47ms across one to 48 GPUs. Keeping measured points, reconstructed surfaces and interpretive materials distinguishable enables close inspection of where additional sampling improves a view and where the survey still limits interpretation.
