Speculo: Superposition and Measurement for Long-Context LLMs

Speculo is an open-source persistent data structure for managing speculative context states in long-context LLM serving . The library treats speculative context slices as a probability distribution (a superposition ) and provides seven persistent operations: add_candidate , measure , error_correct , refresh , evict_stale , collapse_to , and branch . Speculo is a classical analogue of quantum superposition — not a quantum computer — and a persistent state for the LLM context, not a streaming KV cache . The book documents the design, the Python reference implementation (2,185 LOC, 71 tests passing in 2.5s), the verified arXiv-cited competing systems (SpeContext, PRR, LongSpec, Superposition Prompting, SpecPV), and the user-facing API. The book is honest about what is and isn't contributed : novel data structure, not a novel speculation algorithm. Speculo is the third book in a three-book series on persistent data structures for LLM systems, alongside The Persistent Rope (KV cache, DOI 10.5281/zenodo.23241292) and Anduril (agent state, DOI 10.5281/zenodo.23265187). The three books together cover every persistent state in an LLM system. Apache 2.0. Source at github.com/lordxmen2k/speculo-context-processing
