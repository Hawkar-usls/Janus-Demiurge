Data and code: Cultural adaptation in mindfulness-based interventions for depressive symptoms in Asian adolescents, a rapid systematic review of reporting and a meta-analysis

Extracted data, screening decisions (model and blinded human calibration), search logs, analysis code (R and Python) and Supplementary Material for a rapid systematic review of how controlled trials of mindfulness-based interventions for depressive symptoms in Asian and ethnic minority adolescents report cultural adaptation, with a meta-analysis. Registered protocol and updates: https://doi.org/10.17605/OSF.IO/5X38H. Version 2 (27 November 2026) accompanies manuscript draft 9 (after five rounds of pre-submission review). Principal estimate (post hoc): six randomised trials without data-integrity flags, g = 0.83 [0.41, 1.26]; registered primary estimate g = 1.16 [0.33, 1.99]. Full-text assessment of 85 disputed model exclusions, retrieval of unread eligible reports and independent human coding of adaptation are pending; worksheets are in calibration/. A language model (Claude Opus 5.5, Anthropic) performed first-pass screening, extraction, coding and the analyses. The authors inspected its exclusions, full-text decisions and extracted values with its decisions visible; the only step reproduced independently by humans is a blinded calibration sample of 127 records, in which the authors agreed with the model at chance level. See README.md and Supplementary Table S17.
