Cardiac Insight AI: An Intelligent Cardiovascular Risk Assessment and Health Education System

Cardiac Insight AI is a web-based academic prototype designed to support preliminary cardiovascular risk assessment and heart-health awareness. The application collects demographic, physiological, laboratory, electrocardiographic, and lifestyle information through a structured patient assessment form. It maps selected clinical inputs to the 13 attributes used by the Cleveland Heart Disease dataset and produces a risk indication using a weighted scoring inference routine. A separate Framingham-inspired heuristic estimates a 10-year cardiovascular risk category. The application also calculates body mass index (BMI), mean arterial pressure, and cholesterol-to-HDL ratio; identifies contributing risk factors; and presents preventive lifestyle recommendations and educational material. Users can review assessment history and export records. The supplied project archive contains evaluation metadata reporting a Random Forest benchmark on 303 Cleveland records; however, the included runtime prediction endpoint implements a standardized weighted-logit calculation rather than loading a Random Forest model. Therefore, the reported benchmark metrics are presented as bundled evaluation metadata and not as proof of live clinical performance. The system is intended for educational screening and project demonstration only, and it does not replace diagnosis or advice from qualified healthcare professionals.
