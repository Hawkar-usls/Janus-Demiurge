Full Rank Reversal Between Right and Inverse Left Perron Eigenvectors for Four Alternatives (Resolution of Bozóki–Csató, arXiv:2603.24274)

We resolve the open question of Bozóki and Csató (arXiv:2603.24274, Section 5, Example 5) affirmatively by constructing an explicit 4×4 rational pairwise comparison matrix with Perron eigenvalue 5 whose positive right eigenvector induces the strict ranking 1 ≻ 2 ≻ 3 ≻ 4 while the entrywise reciprocal of its positive left eigenvector induces the strictly opposite ranking 4 ≻ 3 ≻ 2 ≻ 1. We also characterize the full-reversal class via diagonal rescaling to constant-row-sum pairwise comparison matrices and formalize Saaty's n = 3 sharpness theorem in Lean 4.
