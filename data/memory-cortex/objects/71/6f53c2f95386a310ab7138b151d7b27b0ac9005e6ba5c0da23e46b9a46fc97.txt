Reproduction package for: Hidden queues behind sensor outages

This reproduction package accompanies the manuscript “Identifiability of hidden freeway queues from boundary counts: initial occupancy, queue density and count drift”. Version 2.0 consolidates the numerical source code, experimental protocols, Colab notebooks, archived results, figures and verification reports. It covers deterministic Godunov counterexamples, NGSIM experiments, count-drift calibration, physics-informed reconstruction, Seattle incident validation and I-24 MOTION screening. Instructions for obtaining and processing external datasets are provided. Restricted third-party trajectory data are not redistributed. The scope and limitations of the reproducibility checks are documented in README.md and VERIFICATION.md. Code: MIT licence. Results and figures: CC BY 4.0.
