Summary data and analysis code for "Partial mismatches and short-term transitions in adults' perceived conditions for action"

This record contains the summary data and analysis code accompanying the study “Partial mismatches and short-term transitions in adults’ perceived conditions for action.” The study used three waves of data from the mainland China adult sample of the Global Flourishing Study (GFS). Five indicators from Waves 1 and 2 assessed perceived capability, freedom to pursue valued goals, availability of help, worry about monthly expenses, and worry about basic needs. Latent transition analysis was used to identify profiles and estimate transitions over approximately eight months. The study also examined whether baseline living arrangements, employment, and household income were associated with subsequent transitions, and whether Wave 2 profiles were associated with anxiety symptoms approximately ten months later. Anxiety symptoms were assessed at Wave 3 using the two-item Generalized Anxiety Disorder scale (GAD-2). The profile and transition analysis included 3,034 participants, the analysis of baseline predictors included 3,012, and the anxiety outcome analysis included 3,017. The archive contains aggregate results for Tables 1–2 and Supplementary Tables S1–S12, together with Python scripts and Mplus model syntax. The README maps the files to the analyses and describes the inputs required to rerun them. Participant-level survey data are available separately through the Global Flourishing Study under its access policy. Source project DOI: 10.17605/OSF.IO/3JTZ8.
