Positive Geometry of Oriented Matroid Strata

This page contains supplementary material for the paper Positive Geometry of Oriented Matroid Strata by Arne Kuhrs and Artyom Lisitsyn. In this paper we study the Grassmannian $\mathrm{Gr}_{k,n}$ together with a union of Plücker divisors, and ask whether the regions it cuts out have canonical forms. For the full Plücker boundary, the regions are realization spaces of uniform oriented matroids, and the question concerns the configuration space $\mathcal X_{k,n}$ of $n$ points in $\mathbb P^{k-1}$. In rank three we show that $\mathcal X_{3,n}$ has genus zero for $6 \le n \le 9$, that the canonical forms of the regions span all logarithmic forms for seven points but not for eight, and that the genus becomes positive for ten points because a K3 surface appears in the boundary. For smaller boundaries we give a recursion in $n$ for every $k$, which recovers the positive Grassmannian and applies to realization spaces of stacked polytopes. Each zip file below contains one computation, with a README that describes what is checked, how to run it, and how its notation matches the paper. X37_real_rank.zip (SageMath; about two minutes and 2.5 GB of memory to build $B$, about one minute and 2 GB for its rank). The incidence matrix $B$ over $\mathbb F_2$ with one row for each of the $27\,240$ regions of $\mathcal X_{3,7}$ (reorientation classes $\chi$ of uniform chirotopes of rank $3$ on seven elements) and one column for each of the $211\,365$ Plücker cones $P$ of dimension $6$ of the Dressian, with $B_{\chi,P}=1$ iff $P\subseteq\operatorname{Dr}^\chi(3,7)$. Modulo $2$, $B$ is the matrix of iterated boundaries of the regions at the corners of a tropical compactification, one corner for each Plücker cone. The matrix is included as `B37.npz`. `get_ae_data.sh` downloads the chirotropical Dressians of Antolini and Early and checks them against SHA-256 checksums, `build_B.sage` rebuilds `B37.npz` from them, and `rank_B.sage` computes $\operatorname{rank}_{\mathbb F_2}B=7470$, which gives $\operatorname{cr}_{\mathbb R}(\mathcal X_{3,7})=\operatorname{cr}(\mathcal X_{3,7})=7470$ (section "The real combinatorial rank"). X310_log_form.zip (Julia 1.12, Oscar 1.8.2; about a minute). An exact verification of the telescoping factorization of an explicit $12$-form $\omega$ on $\mathcal X_{3,10}$ along Sturmfels' construction sequence, and of its iterated residue $\pm\, du\wedge da/\partial_b F$ along the ten divisors of Sink's line configuration, the holomorphic $2$-form of Sink's K3 surface (Section 5, "Positive genus for ten and more points", Remark 5.2). The paper expects $\omega$ to be logarithmic and to lie in the kernel of the Brown–Dupont $R$-map, and the README sketches why; the paper's proof that $g(\mathcal X_{3,10}) \ge 1$ (Theorem 5.1) does not use this computation.
