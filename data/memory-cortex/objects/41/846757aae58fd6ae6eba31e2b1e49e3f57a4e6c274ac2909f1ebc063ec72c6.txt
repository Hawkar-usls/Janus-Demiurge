Code and supplementary results for Regime-Dependent Benchmark of Long-Channel-Only Systemic Correction in fNIRS Without Short-Separation Channels

Analysis code and derived results supporting Neurophotonics manuscript NPH-260158-1. The primary benchmark evaluates recovery of paired HbO/HbR responses injected in optical density before preprocessing into resting-state recordings. The archive includes participant-level estimates, preprocessing calibration, both denominator grids, active-channel-fraction and HbR-delay sensitivity analyses, event-timing checks, task-data descriptors, and a separate HbO synthetic experiment. The record contains NPH-260158-1_Code_and_Results.zip and NPH-260158-1_Supplementary_Tables.xlsx. Reproduction instructions and source data links are provided in README.md within the ZIP. The XLSX file contains the results worksheets cited in the manuscript. Original recordings are not included. Version 1.2.3 adds a matched MULPA benchmark without TDDR in all 57 participants, at three doses, for both chromophores and all 11 estimators. Scripts, participant outputs, tables, environment information and validation are in verification/tddr_ablation. The TDDR diagnostic in all 85 resting recordings, task-off fractions and demographic checks remain in verification/response_alignment. Primary estimates, earlier sensitivity outputs, figures and the companion workbook are unchanged from version 1.2.2.
