Reproducibility code, frozen prediction checksums, summary tables, and figures: Promoter DNA methylation and cryptic taurine introgression bound cross-species regulatory prediction in Bos taurus × Bos indicus hybrids

Reproducible computational pipeline, cryptographic prediction checksums (MD5/SHA256), summary and supplementary tables (Tables 1–2 and Tables S1–S4), and vector/raster figures supporting the cross-species benchmark of Enformer (196 kb) and AlphaGenome v2 (1 Mb) against reciprocal Bos taurus × Bos indicus (Angus × Brahman) F1 fetal liver allele-specific expression (GSE148909), paired fetal liver WGBS differentially methylated regions, targeted F1 allele-specific methylation (PRJNA984688), and FAANG bovine liver chromatin maps (GSE158430) on ARS-UCD1.3 and 1000 Bull Genomes Run 8.
