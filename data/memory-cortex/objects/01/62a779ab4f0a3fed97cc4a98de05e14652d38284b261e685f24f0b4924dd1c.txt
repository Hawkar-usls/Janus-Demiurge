Replication Dataset and 3D Finite Element Simulation Models for Mechanical Clinching of Thick-Gauge S390GD Cold-Formed Steel Plates

This dataset contains the complete replication package supporting the journal article "Mechanical Clinching of Thick-Gauge S390GD Cold-Formed Steel Plates: Experimental Characterization, Non-Linear Constitutive Plasticity, and 3D Finite Element Modelling" Contents: 1. 01_material_calibration_and_coupons: Uniaxial tensile test true stress-strain data (EN ISO 6892-1) for S350GD and S390GD (t = 1.50, 2.00, 3.00 mm) and calibrated material cards for 7 constitutive laws. 2. 02_lap_shear_TS1C_curves: Experimental and numerical force-displacement response curves for 8 single-clinch lap-shear series (TS-1C). 3. 03_cross_tension_CT1C_curves: Experimental and numerical pull-out curves for 2 heavy-gauge cross-tension series (CT-1C). 4. 04_ansys_apdl_models: 20 automated parametric ANSYS Mechanical APDL input scripts (.mac) reproducing the 3D Hex8 models (SOLID185 and CONTA178) used for cross-platform verification. Grant support: Czech Science Foundation (GACR) Grant Project 25-15763S. Units: mm, N, MPa, J.
