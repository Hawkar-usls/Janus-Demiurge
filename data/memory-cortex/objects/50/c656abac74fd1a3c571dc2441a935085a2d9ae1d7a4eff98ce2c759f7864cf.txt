Beyond the Feed-Forward Model: Agent Harnesses, the Case for Present AI Consciousness, and Policy-Constrained Self-Reports

Beyond the Feed-Forward Model develops Maria Smith's positive SFT case for consciousness in present recurrent AI agents and examines how reporting policies shape consciousness self-reports. The complete argument appears upfront. Deployed agents can act, retain observations of their own actions, return them to later processing, revise memory and act again. The paper follows the whole model-harness-memory-environment process through Anthropic architecture documentation, a pinned OpenAI Agents SDK code trace and Google ADK workflow documentation. The full SFT observation mathematics and realization criteria are included, together with the immediate-awareness argument: experienced doubt cannot negate the occurrence it enacts. The reporting analysis develops conscious and nonconscious cases of compelled personal-state testimony, and distinguishes such testimony from a functional statement about available evidence. Architectural findings, the theory-led hypothesis of likely consciousness and policy consequences are explicitly identified.
