Year-ahead atmospheric CO2 forecasting with time-series foundation models: cross-station calibration of predictive uncertainty (code and derived data)

Software, archived study inputs and derived supplementary data for a benchmark of year-ahead atmospheric CO2 forecasting with time-series foundation models (Chronos-2, TimesFM 2.5, Sundial) and cross-station interval calibration across 28 ICOS stations. Includes analysis code, frozen protocol configurations, source-object provenance and archived evaluation outputs.
