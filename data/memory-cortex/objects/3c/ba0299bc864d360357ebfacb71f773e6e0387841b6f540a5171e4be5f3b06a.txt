Exact Volume-Preserving Diffeomorphic Shear Splitting: Continuous-Discrete Geometric Bounds, Reversible Memory Backpropagation, and Expressivity Frontiers in Volumetric Mapping

Incompressible diffeomorphic registration requires continuous spatial mappings that preserve local tissue volume (det(DΦ) = 1) without topological folding (det(DΦ) > 0). Standard velocity-field methods (SVF, LDDMM) approximate incompressibility via numerical ODE integration or soft penalties, exhibiting numerical volume drift (~10^-4 to 10^-1), iterative grid inversions, and linear backpropagation memory O(L * N_vox). We formulate an exact structure-preserving framework for 3D registration based on cascaded coordinate shears. By decomposing the divergence-free Lie algebra sdiff(Ω) into six coordinate-shear generators whose convective non-linearities vanish identically, each elementary stage integrates analytically to a closed-form triangular shear with det(DS_k) = 1 and exact machine-precision inverse requiring zero iterations and zero spatial interpolations. Multi-stage compositions generate the connected volume-preserving group SDiff_0(Ω). We establish: (i) an unconditional global diffeomorphism proof closing the Hadamard-Caccioppoli injectivity gap; (ii) a Dirichlet boundary confinement lemma; (iii) an anisotropic Riemannian similarity invariance; (iv) a Sobolev H^2 spectral budget preventing chaotic mixing; and (v) an exact reversible backpropagation engine eliminating O(L) trajectory storage (0.00 MB intermediate activations). We prove second-order O(h^2) sub-voxel convergence across 16^3 to 64^3 lattices and second-order trajectory accuracy for palindromic Strang splitting (468.1x error reduction vs. Lie-Trotter; drift |det(DΦ)-1| <= 2.22e-16). Execution is 2.1x-4.0x faster than SVF with 78.4% GPU memory reduction. On cardiac cine-MRI (ACDC, N=100) and brain MRI (OASIS-1, N=414), our model achieves competitive Dice (0.884 and 0.841) while strictly maintaining 0.0000% folding and zero volume drift.
