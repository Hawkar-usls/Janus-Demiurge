Snow freshwater flux calculation scripts

Changes for v1.1.1: Uncertainty calculation fixes, other minor fixes Code for snow freshwater flux calculation, including output data; refer to README.md for additional information and full references. Archived from GitHub. Makes use of the following datasets: Sea ice fluxes from the RADARSAT Constellation Mission and Sentinel-1 (Howell et al., 2022): https://catalogue.ec.gc.ca/geonetwork/srv/api/records/09ab22c4-e211-4d23-afe6-9e7aa6831c62 Ice volume flux calculated in the above using version 2.0 of the Landy et al. (2022) year-round sea ice thickness data (Landy et al., 2026): https://data.npolar.no/dataset/6581f94e-d88d-4237-a555-712d19b1e527 Snow datasets used (article references + dataset doi): NESOSIM: NASA Eulerian Snow On Sea Ice Model, v1.1 (Petty et al., 2018): https://zenodo.org/records/18273881 SnowModel-LG: Lagrangian Snow Distributions for Sea Ice Applications, version 1 (Liston et al., 2020; Stroeve et al., 2020): https://nsidc.org/data/nsidc-0758/versions/1 5VLSTM: 25 km resolution snow depth on Arctic sea ice dataset (2012-2020) (Li et al., 2022): https://data.tpdc.ac.cn/zh-hans/data/8b4b5f67-ee01-45ee-96a6-db8f712a2e0c LEGOS: Multi-frequency altimetry snow depth product over Arctic sea ice (Carret et al., 2025): https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.14000765 NaRRS: Nadir Radiometer and Radar Synergy for Arctic Snow Depth and Sea Ice Thickness Retrieval (Nelson et al., 2026): https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.17942492 Sea ice age: EASE-Grid Sea Ice Age, Version 4 (Tschudi et al., 2019): https://doi.org/10.5067/UTAV7490FEPB Snow salinity: From observational data from Nandan et al. (2017): https://doi.org/10.1002/2017GL074506
