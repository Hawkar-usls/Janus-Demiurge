Zenodo research description This second Classic 777 family connects extracellular matrix constraints with neurotrophic cue gradients. F011–F015 examine composition, proteoglycan barriers, scar transport, dry-mass remodeling and topographic guidance; F016–F020 distinguish directional gradients, equilibrium occupancy, factorial interactions, local retention and off-target connectivity. The coupling is a proposed evidence architecture: quantities pass between studies only with an independent identity, unit, reference, uncertainty and veto. Ten books explore 100 subtopics through 300 candidate equation entries, 82 chapters per book, and 200 Research Gates per book. This work extends the master blueprint rather than reporting completed neural regeneration experiments. The comparison of form and function is an explicit scientific requirement, not an assumption. The five-stage inquiry proceeds from physical origin to mathematical evolution, rival counterfactuals, extreme offline extrapolation, and evidence convergence; a sixth page in each chapter preserves independent research inheritance. Structural measurements, concentration maps, and binding proxies are kept separate from functional reconnection. The candidate relations are standard or conditional analytical tools and are not new validated laws. No human intervention, animal intervention, or live biological manipulation is proposed in this working edition.
