Zenodo research description This third Classic 777 family connects the transport of oxygen and metabolic substrates with mechanical constraints on regenerative niches. F021–F025 distinguish diffusive flux, oxygen-history and lineage accounting, glucose–lactate exchange, avascular diffusion times and supply-demand margins; F026–F030 investigate elastic modulus, viscoelastic relaxation, section stress, connected pore space and graded interfaces. The coupling is a proposed evidence architecture: quantities pass between studies only with independent identity, unit, reference, uncertainty and veto. Ten books explore 100 subtopics through 300 candidate equation entries, 82 chapters per book, and 200 Research Gates per book. This work extends the master blueprint rather than reporting completed neural regeneration experiments. The five-stage inquiry proceeds from physical origin to mathematical evolution, rival counterfactuals, extreme offline extrapolation, and evidence convergence; a sixth page in each chapter preserves independent research inheritance. Structural measurements, concentration maps, and mechanical proxies are kept separate from functional reconnection. The candidate relations are standard or conditional analytical tools and are not new validated laws. No human intervention, animal intervention, or live biological manipulation is proposed in this working edition.
