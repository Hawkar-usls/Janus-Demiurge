PaleoSTeHM Dashboard: Interactive STGP Holocene Sea-Level Modeling Framework

Interactive Streamlit dashboard and modular spatiotemporal Gaussian Process (STGP) framework for Holocene relative sea-level (RSL) reconstruction, with optional 1D/3D Glacial Isostatic Adjustment (GIA) priors, kernel decomposition, sea-level budget analysis, and sensitivity testing. Built on the upstream PaleoSTeHM (PSTHM) library.
