Replication package for "Valuing Wind Schedule Improvements under Plant Penalties and Organization Level Netting"

Inputs, scripts and reference outputs for reproducing the analysis in the article "Valuing Wind Schedule Improvements under Plant Penalties and Organization Level Netting". The package starts from archived hourly plant-level panels and published price series for Türkiye (EPİAŞ Transparency Platform; USD buying rate from the Central Bank of the Republic of Türkiye) and runs offline. See README.md in the archive for inputs, run order and verification against the reference outputs.
