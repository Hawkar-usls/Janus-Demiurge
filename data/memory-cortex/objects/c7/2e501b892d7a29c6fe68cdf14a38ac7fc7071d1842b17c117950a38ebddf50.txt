EDUCATION RESOURCE CENTRE (ERC): FOR IMPROVING QUALITY EDUCATION AND SUSTAINABLE NATIONAL DEVELOPMENT IN NIGERIA

This paper focused on the efficiency of Education Resource Centres (ERC) as a vital educational ingredient to teaching-learning process anywhere in the world especially in Nigeria. ERC helps in the training of in-service teachers as well as professionals. Taking students to ERC for practical purpose reduces verbalism and abstraction making discovered facts stick firmly to the memory of audience. However, there is an outcry in the state of ERCs in Nigeria as teaching-learning machines, tools, gadgets and equipments in the country’s ERCs today is no longer supporting theories and research due to their deficiencies to measure accurately what they purport to measure in accordance to the standard metric unit (SI unit) where it claims to exist. The purpose of this study therefore, was to examine the efficiency of ERC in improving quality education towards sustainable national development in Nigeria. The paper is therefore discussed under the following headings: the concept of ERC, education for sustainable national development, quality education, goals and objectives in Education, the place of ERC in sustainable national development, present status of ERC in Nigeria, the taxonomy of ERCs and their potent functions, the factors affecting the establishment of ERC in Nigeria which include fund for the acquisition of modern teaching-learning machines that supports modern day theories and practices, poor policy or lack of policy implementation and practice were exermined. Five solutions were suggested which includes: Positive educational policies should be fully implemented and Government at all levels in partnership with hosting communities should set up proactive monitoring team that will supervise and evaluate the procurement and daily operational routine of the centre. The paper concludes by making six recommendations which include; the Government should make adequate funds available for the day-to-day running of ERC Centres and also, ensures that the funds are judiciously utilized on what it was funded.
