D1.1 Methodology: baseline data, monitoring, evaluation and demo planning and design

Deliverable 1.1 outlines the methodology for baseline data collection, monitoring, evaluation, and demonstration site planning within the PHAROS project. It establishes a structured five-step framework to ensure the effectiveness of marine ecosystem restoration efforts through standardised data collection, performance tracking, and adaptive planning. This deliverable serves as the foundation for future implementation, providing a replicable approach to assessing biodiversity, pollution reduction, and socio-economic impacts in alignment with Mission Ocean objectives.
