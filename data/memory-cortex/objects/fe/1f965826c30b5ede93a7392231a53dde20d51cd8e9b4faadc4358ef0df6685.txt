DEVELOPMENT OF AN AI-BASED EDUCATIONAL CHATBOT FOR ENHANCING SCHOOL STUDENTS' KNOWLEDGE

Personalized learning remains a persistent challenge in secondary education due to high student-to-teacher ratios and heterogeneous knowledge baselines among pupils. While Large Language Models (LLMs) offer conversational tutoring capabilities, unconstrained generative models frequently produce factual hallucinations and provide direct homework answers rather than pedagogical scaffolding. This paper presents the design, mathematical formulation, and software implementation of an AI-based educational chatbot utilizing Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) and Bayesian Knowledge Tracing (BKT) to enhance secondary school students' academic mastery in STEM subjects (Mathematics, Physics, and Computer Science). The system integrates a vector database (FAISS) indexing state-approved school textbooks with a Socratic dialogue manager accessible via web and Telegram interfaces. A 12-week pedagogical experiment involving N = 160 secondary school students (grades 8–10), divided equally into an Experimental Group (N = 80) and a Control Group (N = 80), demonstrated a statistically significant improvement in post-test academic performance (+23.4% mean score increase, p < 0.001, Cohen's d = 1.18) and a 61.5% reduction in homework completion latency.
