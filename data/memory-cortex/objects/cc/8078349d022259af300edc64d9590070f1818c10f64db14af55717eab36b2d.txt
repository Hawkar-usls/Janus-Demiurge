Geometry at Rest, in Motion, and in Memory

We present a minimal three-layer framework in which spacetime geometry emerges from a pre-geometric sub-Planckian microscopic substrate. The microscopic layer consists of algebraic tokens evolving under local update rules. A resolution kernel performs coarse-graining, producing macroscopic fields that admit a geometric interpretation. Geometry appears as a bloom: a stable, self-consistent structure arising from averaged microscopic algebraic token behavior. In this setting, curvature corresponds to the macroscopic imprint of microscopic motion, while torsion reflects the macroscopic memory of intrinsic spin alignment. Torsion is algebraic, non-propagating, and carries no independent degrees of freedom. Mass is identified with geometry at rest.
