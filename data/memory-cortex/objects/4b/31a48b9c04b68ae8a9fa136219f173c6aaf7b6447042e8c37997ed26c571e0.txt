dypole: evaluation of solid-state NMR spin-echo decay, REDOR/RESPDOR and relaxation experiments

First archived release of dypole, used for the 23Na and 7Li spin-echo decay (SED) evaluation in Structural memory and metastable ionic transport in silicate glasses produced by sub-Tg reverse ion exchange . Echo-aligned SED evaluation (Dataset.sed_eval(fid='echo')), repeated measurements (sed_replicates) and stitching of series with different echo-delay lists (sed_stitch) Installable package (pip install .), example scripts, and tests with a regression baseline for the SED results Bruker delays read from vdlist; fixes in T1, T1rho, REDOR/RESPDOR and SAE evaluations; frequency-axis length fix in spectral processing REDOR/RESPDOR, T1, T1rho and SAE evaluations are experimental.
