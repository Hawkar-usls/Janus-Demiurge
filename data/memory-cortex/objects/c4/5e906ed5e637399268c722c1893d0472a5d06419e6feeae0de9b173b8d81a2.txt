Computational data for "Elastic backscattering at a Pd adatom on a metallic (6,6) carbon nanotube before and after molecular hydrogen adsorption"

Quantum ESPRESSO/PWCOND calculations of elastic backscattering by a Pd adatom on a metallic (6,6) carbon nanotube, with and without molecular hydrogen. The archive includes paired complex reflection and transmission amplitudes for two spin blocks and two projection thresholds, input structures, selected calculation outputs, and data for the adsorption-site, electronic-smearing, dispersion and dissociated-hydrogen tests. The calculation files are accompanied by a data guide and checksums. The QE source overlay retains its original software license. Download the main ZIP and README to inspect the numerical results. Three bzip2 files contain the full orbital-projection logs for bare Pd/CNT, molecular H₂, and dissociated 2H. Their extraction paths and SHA-256 checksums are in spectra/dos/RESTORE_PROJECTION_LOGS.txt inside the ZIP. Version 2 adds Two_impurity_model.zip, which contains the inputs, scripts, energy-resolved reflection data and numerical checks for the two-impurity tight-binding model. The model compares impurity separations of 8 and 12 primitive periods along the nanotube using single-defect reflection data for bare Pd and for Pd with adsorbed H₂. The files reproduce Fig. 3 of the revised manuscript and Table S8 of the Supplementary Information.
