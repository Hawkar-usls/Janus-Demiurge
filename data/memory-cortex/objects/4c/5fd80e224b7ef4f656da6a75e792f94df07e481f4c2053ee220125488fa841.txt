What Counts as Oceanic Horror?

This dataset supports an exposure-weighted study of oceanic horror in contemporary video games. It combines a full retrieval of all horror-tagged and sea-tagged games in the Steam store (33,183 games) with three exposure measures: estimated owners (SteamSpy), measured play hours 2015–2026 (steamcharts.com) and total user reviews. It contains a sea frame of the 232 sea-tagged games with at least 1,000 reviews (main genre and horror share, to measure horror's place among sea-oriented games), a long-tail corpus of 387 horror games with a sea tag or sea keywords in their store description, and a coded core of 65 high-exposure games. The core has draft codes for the presence and role of the sea (open-water threat, enclosed maritime infrastructure, submerged environment, monster habitat, extractive resource, transit zone, atmospheric backdrop), the threats, the mythic traditions drawn on, and the developer's country and language. Version 0.1 is preliminary: the frames and exposure data are complete, but the codes are unverified drafts with confidence ratings. Weighted results depend heavily on the Subnautica games (72 % of the play hours of the coded oceanic-horror corpus). Data retrieved 23 November 2026. Prepared for the paper "What Counts as Oceanic Horror? An Exposure-Weighted Study of Contemporary Video Games".
